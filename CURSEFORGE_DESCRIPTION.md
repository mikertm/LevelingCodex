# Leveling Codex

**Your leveling guide, inside the game.** Open your talents and the Codex traces the one to take. Level up and a banner shows exactly what you unlocked. One click sets up smart macros for your level, for controller or keyboard.

Built from **The Leveling Codex** guides — one researched leveling spec per class, checked against the WoW: Forever client and hotfixes.

---

## Features

### Talent window guide
Open your talent window and the Codex **traces the talent to take for your level** — a glowing outline with sparks running around it and a *Codex pick 2/3* tag — plus a card above the window with that talent and your next two picks.

### Level-up fanfare
Every level-up, a banner bursts in near the top of the screen: **"You have reached Level 26"** over a turning starburst, with each unlock sliding in with its icon:
- Talent point to take (with rank)
- New spells at your trainer
- New or updated macros
- New guide chapter

Hover to keep it up, click it for the full guide, right-click to dismiss. Move it, resize it or turn it off in Settings, and use **Test level-up** to try it any time.

### The Codex window
Type **/codex** or click the book on your minimap. Browse any level from 1 to 60 with the arrows.
- **This Level** — your talent with the reason for it, the next picks, what to train, and your macros with the exact controller button and keyboard key for each.
- **Talents** — the whole 10–60 order with icons. Hover for why, click to jump.
- **Trainer** — every trainer visit, with icons and notes.
- **Macros** — every macro for your level with its code, its buttons and how it works.
- **Tips** — the play-style chapter for your level range.
- **Settings** — everything below, no slash commands needed.

### One-click smart macros
**Set up macros** creates this character's macros for your level. When you level into a new version (for example a Fight macro that adds Shield Slam at 40), the Codex **updates the macros it made automatically**. Your own macros are never touched.

Every macro is built for Forever's macro rules: it never gets stuck on a spell that's on cooldown, and cast sequences are versioned by level.

### Made for controller players
Forever's gamepad layout is built in: every macro says which trigger set and button it goes on. Choose **Xbox, PlayStation or Switch** button names in Settings.

### Two themes
**Forever** (teal, the default) or **Codex** (gold and parchment).

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/codex` | Open or close the Codex window |
| `/codex macros` | Create or update your macros for your level |
| `/codex test` | Test level-up (`/codex test 30` for any level) |
| `/codex minimap` | Show or hide the minimap button |
| `/codex reset` | Reset the window, banner and minimap button positions |

Left-click the minimap book to open the Codex, right-click for Settings, and drag it around the minimap edge. You can also bind a key or controller button to open the Codex (Key Bindings > AddOns), and close it with Esc or B.

Every macro card has a **Copy** button, and if your macro slots are full the Codex opens the macro text ready to copy, so you can make it yourself.
