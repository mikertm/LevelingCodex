# Leveling Codex

**Your leveling guide, inside the game.** A step-by-step quest route for **Alliance and Horde, levels 1-30**, with a waypoint arrow, dungeon checklists and class quests. Open your talents and the Codex traces the one to take. Level up and a card shows exactly what you unlocked. One click sets up smart macros for your level, for controller or keyboard. And all **12 professions** have a guide from 1 to 300.

Built from **The Leveling Codex** guides — one researched leveling spec per class, checked against the WoW: Forever client and hotfixes.

---

## Features

### Step-by-step quest guide, levels 1-30
A small step window tells you exactly what to do next — accept, kill, loot, turn in, fly, hearth, train — with an arrow that points the way. It reads your quest log, so it always picks up where you are.

- **Every race starts at home.** Alliance: Northshire, Coldridge Valley, Shadowglen, or Zephras Isle (High Order Skyborne). Horde: the Valley of Trials, Camp Narache, Deathknell, or Zephras Isle (Windshaper Skyborne).
- **Then one shared route per faction.**
  - Alliance: Westfall, Lakeshire, Duskwood and Forever's reworked Wetlands.
  - Horde: the Crossroads and the Barrens, Stonetalon, Ashenvale, the Sepulcher and Hillsbrad, then Thousand Needles to Freewind Post.
- **Built for WoW: Forever.** The new starting-zone quests, the Camping intros, the new class letters (Orc Mage, Troll Warlock, Undead Paladin and more), and Forever's new quests in every zone. Quests are grouped into tight loops around each town, so you hand in everything on one pass.
- **Alternate routes** when you want a change of scenery:
  - Alliance: Loch Modan, Darkshore, Ashenvale, Stonetalon, the early Wetlands, Southshore, and late Redridge and Duskwood.
  - Horde: Tirisfal and Silverpine for Kalimdor races at 12-15, and the Shimmering Flats.

  The Quests tab lists the route options for your level.
- **The arrow** shows the distance on your map and knows the way across zones: flight paths, boats, zeppelins and roads. It pauses during flights and picks up when you land.
- **Quest item and target buttons** for the current step, both key-bindable: use the quest item, or target the NPC you're looking for.
- **Catch-up built in.**
  - Missed pickups move to Missed quests.
  - Outleveled pickups leave the route, unless a later quest needs them.
  - Breadcrumbs you've already used drop out.
  - *Also on this loop* shows the other quests you can work on during the same trip.
  - Steps show a quest's place in its chain ("Chain: part 2 of 5").
  - A pickup warns you when your quest log is full.
- **Dungeon plans.** Plan the runs you want: Ragefire Chasm, Wailing Caverns, Shadowfang Keep, Blackfathom Deeps, the Deadmines, the Stockade, Razorfen Kraul, Gnomeregan, and Forever's Ruins of Lordaeron, Hall of Thanes and Excavation Site.
  - A planned run's pickups join your route.
  - When you're ready, the step window offers **Run <dungeon>**.
  - Each run has a before / inside / after checklist with the bosses in order and a tip for each.
- **Class quests** for every class: stances, forms, totems, pets, Voidwalkers and Succubi, Redemption, poisons and class gear. Short ones sit on your route. Longer chains are class guides in the Quests tab, and you return to leveling when you're done.
- **Special chains** (Alliance) such as the Cozy Sleeping Bag, The Legend of Stalvan and The Missing Diplomat are offered when you pass their start.
- **Auto-accept and auto-turn-in** for the guide's quest, off by default. Hold Alt to do it yourself. Reward choices always stay yours.
- **Your settings:** show optional quests, plan every dungeon run, show class quests, keep grey quests in the route, skip hearthstone binding steps, play a sound when a step completes, and hide the arrow or buttons.

### Talent window guide
Whenever you have a talent point to spend, open your talent window and the Codex **traces the talent to take for your level**. It draws a glowing outline in the theme colour that follows the talent's shape (round, square or choice), with sparks running around it and a *Codex pick 2/3* tag. A card above the window (or below it, near the top of the screen) shows that talent and your next two picks. With several points to spend it starts with the earliest, and it goes away once they're spent.

### What's new at each level
After the game's own level-up display, a **"New at Level 26"** card takes its place on screen, with each unlock sliding in with its icon:
- Talent point to take (with rank)
- New spells at your trainer
- New or updated macros
- New guide chapter
- Profession ranks you can now train

Hover to keep it up, click it for the full guide, right-click to dismiss. Open the Codex within a minute of the card and it opens on that level. Move it, resize it or turn it off in Settings, and use **Test level-up** there to try it any time.

### The Codex window
Type **/codex**, click the **Level 60 badge** on your minimap, or pick the Codex in the addons button under the clock. Browse any level from 1 to 60 with the page arrows. The window uses the game's own frame art: gold title banner, your class portrait and character-sheet tabs.
- **This Level** — your talent with the reason for it, the next picks, what to train, and your macros with the exact controller button and keyboard key for each.
- **Talents** — a talent tree with your build filled in up to the level you're viewing (gold when maxed, green when partly taken, later points marked with their level). Click a talent for every point that goes into it and why. Once you've opened your talent window, the tree matches the game's layout. Below it, every point from 10 to 60 in order.
- **Trainer** — every trainer visit, with icons and notes.
- **Macros** — every macro for your level with its code and how it works, plus your controller's button sets (R2, L2, L2 + R2...) and an action bar showing where each one goes.
- **Tips** — the play-style chapter for your level range.
- **Quests** — the current step, the route ahead, route options for your level, missed quests, and the guide library. The library has every leveling route, dungeon and class guide, searchable by quest name or ID.
- **Professions** — the guide for each profession you know (see below).
- **Settings** — everything, no slash commands needed.

### One-click smart macros
**Set up macros** creates this character's macros for your level. When you level into a new version (for example a Fight macro that adds Shield Slam at 40), the Codex **updates the macros it made automatically**. Your own macros are never touched, even one with the same name.

Every macro is built for Forever's macro rules: it never gets stuck on a spell that's on cooldown, and cast sequences are versioned by level. Every macro card has a **Copy** button, and if your macro slots are full the Codex opens the macro text ready to copy.

### Made for controller players
Forever's gamepad layout is built in: every macro says which trigger set and button it goes on, drawn as the game's button glyphs. Choose **Xbox, PlayStation or Switch** buttons in Settings.

The whole Codex window works on the controller with Forever's own navigation:
- **D-pad** to move and **A** to press.
- **B**, Back or Esc to close.
- **LB/RB** to switch tabs and the **right stick** to scroll.

The Quests tab has the step controls (previous, next, hide), and the step window leaves your controller free for playing. If Forever's navigation misbehaves, Settings > Controller switches to the Codex's own highlight. Bind a key or controller button to open the Codex (Key Bindings > AddOns).

### Profession guides: all 12 professions, 1-300
Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Enchanting, Engineering, First Aid, Fishing, Herbalism, Leatherworking, Mining, Skinning and Tailoring. Built for WoW: Forever's reworked professions, from the game's own recipe colours and reagents.
- **Professions tab** for every profession you know:
  - your skill and the recipe to make now, with how many more and the materials against your bags;
  - where to learn it, and the trainer, book or quest for the next rank when it's due;
  - the whole route and a shopping list to 300.
- **Profession extras:**
  - Mining, Herbalism and Skinning show what to gather where for your faction.
  - Tailoring counts the shopping list in raw cloth and shows which mobs and zones drop each cloth.
  - Enchanting says which rod each step needs and what each green or blue item in your bags would disenchant into.
  - Fishing shows where to catch Nat Pagle's quest fish.
- **Crafting window guide**: open your crafting window and the Codex outlines the recipe to make, with a card showing how many more and how many you have materials for. It does a part first when your step needs one: bolts of cloth, a missing rod, Dust to Motes.

  It never touches the window itself: scroll the list as usual (mouse or controller) and the recipe is outlined when it comes into view.
- **Skill-up card** when you reach a new step, can train the next rank, or can gather something new. Open the Codex within a minute of it and it opens on that profession.
- Turn the crafting window guide or the skill-up cards off under Settings > Professions.

### Gathering tracking stays on
WoW: Forever turns **Find Herbs** and **Find Minerals** off when you log in, zone or die. The Codex turns the one you had back on for you (out of combat, never over another tracking such as Track Beasts), and turns it on when you learn Herbalism or Mining. Untick it in Settings if you'd rather do it yourself.

### Two themes
**Forever** (teal, the default) or **Codex** (gold and parchment).

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/codex` | Open or close the Codex window |
| `/codex quests` | Open the Quests tab and show the step window |
| `/codex macros` | Create or update your macros for your level |
| `/codex test` | Test level-up (`/codex test 30` for any level) |
| `/codex minimap` | Show or hide the minimap button |
| `/codex reset` | Reset the window, banner and minimap button positions |
| `/codex debug` | List what the Codex can see, to paste into a bug report (also quest API checks and trainer requirements that differ from the profession guide) |

Left-click the minimap badge to open the Codex, right-click for Settings, and drag it around the minimap edge.
