## Why

The owner played the Wetlands and found two problems: a quest standing next to an NPC the route already visits, and a pickup placed after a walk through its own objective area. This pass looks for the same two problems in the Horde starting zones.

## What was wrong

1. **Kron's Amulet, the last zig-zag in Durotar.** Misha's Lost But Not Forgotten (816) is picked up on the first trip north. The crocolisks were hunted at the very end: from Orgrimmar's gate south-west to the bridge, then back north-east to Misha, then south-west again to the bridge. That is about 1,240 yd of back and forth. The crocolisks also live on the river right below Thunder Ridge, where the second trip north already hunts Lightning Hides. Bind on Forever places them there too. Guide A has the same end detour, for its Hunters and Shamans only.
2. **Thunder Bluff, third visit.** The route went lift, east to Cairne, back west to Eyahn and Boarton, south-east to Hunter Rise, then back west to the lift.
3. **Orgrimmar, second visit.** After the streams in the east of the Valley of Honor, the route went to Kor'geld, Yelmak and Whuut, then back south-east to Kamari (Halikor's Hoof, and the Hunter letter for Ormak), then all the way west to Grommash Hold.
4. **Brill.** On the second Brill visit, Gretchen's The Chill of Death was picked up next to Coleman Farthing, who already offers 354 and 362 at that level. Those two waited for a third visit. That third visit also had a separate stop at Abigail Shiel to buy one Coarse Thread.
5. **Missing prerequisite.** Sting of the Scorpid (789) had no prerequisite in our data, but it needs Cutting Teeth (788). Because of that, the loop check reported a false late pickup for Warlocks.
6. **Gaps.**
   - Durotar had none of the optional gathering lessons (Camping 101) that Mulgore and Tirisfal offer. The Zygor guide takes the Skinning one.
   - Eylah Sunhorn's Traditions of the Bluff stands beside Eyahn Eagletalon, whom the route visits twice.
