# Alliance dungeon detours through level 30

The detours separate outdoor preparation, in-instance objectives and turn-ins after the run. The Deadmines detour reuses the Westfall quest IDs and the Wetlands detour reuses the Rethiel chain. Completed quests and quests already in the log carry across guides through those IDs. The level ranges describe when to plan a run. Each quest can have a different minimum, and the game controls entry and offers.

Quest and map IDs were checked against the repository's Forever client reference, build 1.60.1.70862. Starting-item IDs were checked against that build's ItemSparse table. NPC and objective facts use the pinned QuestieDB records where available, with public walkthroughs for paths and room landmarks. The local Zygor Forever reference helped identify the sequence to verify. The data uses original route text and remains unverified until tested in game. [Blizzard's dungeon announcement](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301455/) · [QuestieDB Forever snapshot](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/tree/0f7d8adc92b28ac6594f4ad8745257f4d9784edf/data/Forever)

## Conflicting or incomplete evidence

- **Hall of Thanes.** Some accounts start with Beldin's referral and others start directly with Farsen. The guide points players to Beldin if the watch is missing and uses the independently supported Dark Iron Map hand-in for the Incursion prerequisite. Check Farsen's current offer after the map and the first usable level for Afadra and Thom. [Played route](https://dlonem.com/wow-forever/dungeons/hall-of-thanes/) · [Map-to-Incursion chain](https://azeroth.wiki/forever/guides/hall-of-thanes)
- **Ruins of Lordaeron.** The old local reference lists the letter sequel as 35161. The Forever records and cached client instead have 95161. ItemSparse maps 268535 and 268579 to the insignia and crest quests. The letter 251522 has no StartQuestID in that build, so the first quest tells the player to inspect the paper. Sources disagree between levels 15 and 16 for the insignia and crest and place Nightingale at different points. Use the pinned Forever Stormwind location and confirm it live. [Letter sequel](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/95161/) · [Item and NPC records](https://classicwowforever.com/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron/) · [Reported level 16 offers](https://1mw.io/en/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron/quests)
- **The Deadmines and Stockade.** The existing Westfall route offers Underground Assault directly from Shoni. The Ironforge introduction Speak with Shoni 2041 is documented as a referral. Verify the direct offer, the explosives placement and the 373 → 389 → 391 handoff in game. [Shoni quest series](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/series/2041/) · [Stockade handoffs](https://hearthproof.com/dungeons/the-stockade/)
- **Wailing Caverns.** The local reference accepts either 3366 or 6981 for The Glowing Shard. In the cached client, the shard 10441 starts 6981, and the Forever quest record uses it. Check the Ratchet conversation and Darnassus follow-up. [Glowing Shard record](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/6981/)
- **Blackfathom Deeps.** One general list carries the Season of Discovery Heart of the Void pearl quest into Forever. The local guide lists it in a separate raid route, and the pinned Forever ItemSparse has no starter for 78916. Check a live five-player clear before adding a tracked pearl quest. [Reported dungeon association](https://bytetavern.com/wow-forever-dungeon-quests/#blackfathom-deeps-quests-level-24)
- **Excavation Site.** The 26–31 run range follows the October beta update. Highland Hides' offer has been linked to Young Crocolisk Skins and to Daily Delivery, so neither is a hard-coded prerequisite. Seeking Caitlin 95737 is a breadcrumb for players already in Astranaar. The Dragonmaw chain's verified quest giver is the Horde Deathstalker Agent. Check the four-hide counter, the Rethiel chain and whether Ardin gives Heartwoven directly or through a bag item. [October range history](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/dungeons/) · [Highland Hides conflicting observations](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98815/highland-hides) · [Seeking Caitlin](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95737/seeking-caitlin)
- **City of Dalaran.** Public reports give level 24 or 30 for the Alliance quest offer and describe both keyed shore access and an Alliance route through the city. The guide checks the current offer and uses the key handoff. The Skyborne starter's city travel is separate. Check a Skyborne and another Alliance race at Jacques and Modera, and confirm the shore door with one key holder. [Key quest and Alliance access](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/city-of-dalaran-guide) · [Conflicting level reports](https://wowf.io/en/dungeons/dalaran/guide)
- **Gnomeregan and Razorfen Kraul.** Sources differ on the minimums for Kernobee and Willix, so the escort pickup steps defer to the live offer. Check terminal 3005-C at the Electrocutioner platform and the two outside phial trips, then confirm the journal pickup and Willix turn-in after the escort. [Gnomeregan quests](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/gnomeregan-quests) · [Razorfen Kraul quest planning](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/dungeons/razorfen-kraul/quests/)

## In-game checks

1. Open the dungeon from the Library with outdoor preparation already complete on one character and fresh on another. Confirm the active step and the same quest's shared completion state.
2. Check each approach pin and the automatic transition at the portal. The guide uses the Forever instance IDs where they identify a single run. Use Start inside for the Graveyard wing and Dalaran while checking their shared map behavior. The in-instance steps use room landmarks. [Forever Map table](https://wago.tools/db2/Map?build=1.60.1.70862)
3. Verify that item starts stay available once the starter drops, and that a missed starter or disputed offer leaves the other quests usable. Capture the quest and item IDs for any different live offers.
4. Check the after-run steps and class links. Finishing a detour should resume the previously selected outdoor guide.
