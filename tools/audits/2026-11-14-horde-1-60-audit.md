# 2026-11-14: Horde 1-60 loop and gap audit: overview

The owner asked for the whole Horde route to be checked: loops that fit together and are walked smartly, quests the guide misses, optional quests, dungeon chains, and the vendor or Auction House items quests need. Levels 1-30 had a full pass two days earlier (`2026-11-12-1-30-loops-gaps.md` and the three `2026-11-12-horde-*` 1-30 files). The 30-60 route was written in seven parallel parts the same day (`2026-11-12-horde-30-60-integration.md`) and had never been through the loop check. This pass rechecks 1-30 and polishes 30-60. Every chapter is still unverified until played.

## Method

- **Loop check.** `tools/questloops.py HORDE` for all five races and all nine classes, main route and every alternate chapter.
- **Gap list.** Every quest the Horde chapters of the two references accept, against every quest any Horde section names (route, alternates, dungeon, class and special guides):
  - Reference guide A (RXP's Forever guides, the Nov 10 copy): the 1-22 race and Barrens chapters, the 22-60 speedrun and dungeon chapters, the Riverglades I and II chapters and the dungeon guides. Used for quest IDs, grouping, order and positions, never its text. Its world coordinates were turned into map percentages with the client's map extents (`QuestFlightData.lua`).
  - Zygor Forever guides (quest-ID gap check only). No text, order or code taken.
  - Result: 328 quests. 131 are below level 60; the rest are level 60 class, raid, Ahn'Qiraj, cloth-donation and Naxxramas quests (out of scope). Most of the 131 already had a verdict in an earlier audit; each band file says which verdicts changed.
- **Vendor and Auction House.** Every `.collect` buy or Auction House step in reference guide A's Horde chapters and every Zygor `buy` line, against our `buy`/`collect` steps and `QuestShopData.lua`; plus every Horde route quest's QuestieDB item objectives, sorted into vendor-only, vendor or drop, and no source (crafted or Auction House).
- **Three band audits ran in parallel**, each limited to its own file:

| Band | Sections | Audit file |
|---|---|---|
| 30-40 | hillsbrad-30, flats-33, desolace-34, stv-35, dustwallow-37, arathi-38, badlands-39 and the stv-30, desolace-31, dustwallow-33, badlands-37 alternates | `2026-11-14-horde-30-40.md` |
| 40-50 and the Riverglades | stv-40 to feralas-49, the dustwallow-40, searing-45, blasted-47, ungoro-48 alternates, riverglades-rogmar, riverglades-bolderok, riverglades-eastsea, shendralas-pylons | `2026-11-14-horde-40-50.md` |
| 50-60 | blasted-50 to silithus-59 and the seven 50-60 alternates | `2026-11-14-horde-50-60.md` |

Levels 1-30, the dungeon and class guides and the moves between band files were done here.

## Levels 1-30

The remaining gap-list quests all had verdicts from 11-10 and 11-12, except three, now fixed:

- **Cozy Sleeping Bag (79007, 79192, 79980, 79974, 79975, 79976): new Horde special guide** `cozy-sleeping-bag-horde` (end of `QuestHordeBarrens.lua`). The Alliance had one; the Horde had nothing, and the 11-10 audit dropped the chain as "the Westfall swim". The Horde runs it the other way round: the burned watchtower south of Camp Taurajo (Barrens 46.35, 73.89), then Alexston Farmstead in Westfall, the Stonetalon camp and mound, Stonewrought Dam in Loch Modan, and Thoradin's Wall. Positions are the QuestieDB Forever object spawns. The ride to Westfall (zeppelin to Grom'gol, swim north along the coast) uses guide A's coast points, marked `uncertain`. The Loch Modan leg uses the Horde route's own Thandol Span and Dun Algaz points from `badlands-39`. Its 79192 step takes 79007 as prerequisite; the shared quest record keeps the Alliance 79008. `camp-taurajo`'s Gann Stonespire pickup (843, next to the tower) offers the guide (`withGuide`). Sources: [Warcraft Tavern: Cozy Sleeping Bag](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/cozy-sleeping-bag/) (Horde order, level 14 minimum, rewards), the QuestieDB Forever records.
- **Ruins of Lordaeron: A Recipe For Death (447, 450, 451) before The New Plague (95216).** Theodore Griffs only offers The New Plague after Renferrel's three-part chain. Guide A's Ruins of Lordaeron guide runs it as "a pre-quest for The New Plague", and [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron-dungeon-quests) and [In-Game News](https://ingamenews.com/gaming-news/world-of-warcraft-forever-how-to-unlock-the-new-plague-quest/) say the same. QuestieDB Forever has no prerequisite on 95216. Before, the guide sent players to Griffs with nothing to pick up. The guide's preparation now runs the chain:
  - 447 at the Sepulcher: Skittering Blood from the Moss Stalkers and Mist Creepers (35.4, 14.5), then bear hearts (37.5, 16.4).
  - 450 at Faranell in the Undercity: Berard's journal from the bookshelf in Pyrewood (43.0, 73.2).
  - 451 at Renferrel: a Hardened Tumor from the Vile Fins (56.9, 24.9), Lake Skulker Moss (71.7, 35.7) and Lake Creeper Moss (76.4, 22.4).
  - The New Plague from Griffs after the 451 hand-in.
  - Tabitha (92401) is taken on the first Sepulcher visit; the second Sepulcher round trip is gone.
  - Spots are QuestieDB drop clusters.
  - `q(95216, ..., 451)` gives the pickup its prerequisite.
- **Shadowguard (5643)** is the Undercity copy of the 5642 the route takes from Miles Welsh (QuestieDB marks them exclusive), so nothing changes. The Sparklematic (2952) belongs to the Gnomeregan guide.

The 1-30 loop findings are the ten explained in `2026-11-12-1-30-loops-gaps.md` (level gates, escorts and log choices). None is new.
