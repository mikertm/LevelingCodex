# 2026-11-12: Levels 1-30 loop and gap audit (both factions): overview

The owner played the Wetlands 20-25 chapter and found two faults there. Whelgar's Uncovering the Past wasn't in the guide, though he stands next to Merrin. And once added, its pickup came after a walk through the quest's own dig, which meant zig-zagging back (`2026-11-12-wetlands-early-dig.md`). This pass looks for the same faults across every leveling chapter from 1 to 30, for both factions: loops that should be grouped, and quests the guide misses. Every chapter is still unverified until played.

## Method

- **Loop check.** `tools/questloops.py` now walks each alternate chapter as its own path; before, it walked only the main route. It also reports **late pickups**: an accept the route could have taken at an earlier visit to the same spot, when the walk in between passes that quest's objective area. That is the Wetlands dig fault. On the old Wetlands order it flags Whelgar; on the fixed order it is clean.
- **Gap lists.** Three sources were compared with every quest any route step uses (any section):
  - every quest the RXP Forever 1-30 chapters ("guide A") accept: 82 Alliance and 120 Horde quests were missing;
  - the Zygor Forever 1-30 leveling guides: 64 Alliance and 78 Horde missing. Only 5 and 7 of those weren't also on the RXP list, mostly class quests.
  - QuestieDB quests that start in each faction's zones (level 31 or lower).
- **Rules for the references.** Guide A gave quest IDs, grouping, order and positions, never its text. The Zygor guides were used as a quest-ID gap check only, at the owner's request (2026-11-12), with no text, step order or code taken. Web sources (Bind on Forever, games.gg, Warcraft Tavern, Wowhead Forever, questing.wiki) were cross-checked.
- **QuestieDB minimum levels.** Quests were not dropped on QuestieDB's minimum level alone: the game offered 299 at 24, though QuestieDB says 25.
- **Seven area audits ran in parallel**, each limited to its own sections. Each has its own file with sources, a findings table (RXP and Zygor columns), data changes for mirroring into the guide pages, left-out reasons and a check-in-game list:

## Fixes that cross areas (made during the merge)

- **Horde Fly steps sat at the departure flight master** (found by the Horde 22-30 audit). A Fly step belongs at the destination: `Q.flightStep` keeps it current until you land, and the flight-map mark and auto-fly work from it. Placed at the departure, each one counted as done as soon as you reached that flight master, before the flight map opened.
  - The 22-30 file had 13. Another 19 were in the four Horde dungeon guides, Silverpine's bat ride to the Undercity, the Horde Skyborne flight to Orgrimmar, and the Shaman water totem and Warrior Field of Giants trips. All now sit at the destination.
  - `questroute.py audit` now fails any `fly`/`getfp` step more than 60 yd from the flight point it names. It reports 31 failures on the pre-audit data and none now.
- **Catch-up after the early Wetlands chapter** (found by the Duskwood and Wetlands audit).
  - `markPassed` (Quests.lua) treated any turned-in quest from a section as proof you had walked it. A player who had done the 20-25 chapter, which shares most of its quests with the 26-30 route, got Howin's two quests, Blisters on the Land, War Banners, From the Ashes, Apprentice's Duties, Crocs of the Sky and Fall of Dun Modr marked missed. The guide then jumped to The Doomed Fleet in Stormwind.
  - Now a quest two leveling sections pick up counts as progress only for the lower-level section, so the early chapter's turn-ins count for the early chapter alone.
  - New scenario `questWetlandsAfterEarly`: a level 27 at Howin after Duskwood and the early chapter gets Howin's pickup as the current step, with nothing missed. The existing `questWetlandsDig` checks that the early chapter still counts its own progress.
- **Horde Priests:** Shadowguard and Dark Sacrifice are now taken from Miles Welsh while training at Thunder Bluff. Before, they came after Zamah's turn-in, a walk back up the Spirit Rise (`QuestHordeClassCasters.lua`).
- **wetlands-early:** Daily Delivery (469) is taken from Einar at the crossroads on the eastern trip, with a goto first, and handed in to Halloran on the last Menethil visit. The 26-30 route also takes it now.
- `QuestMobData.lua` was regenerated after the merge.

## Merging with main's 30-60 guides

Main gained the 30-60 routes for both factions while this audit ran. The merged data failed the route audit on four counts, all fixed in the merge's follow-up commit:

- **227 Fly steps in the 30-60 files sat at the departure flight master** (the bug above). They now sit at the destination's flight master, found from the route's own correct steps; Farholde Keep, Powderfuse Port and Talrendis Point use the flight point itself. The flight-point check is now faction-aware: nine hubs (Gadgetzan, Everlook, Thorium Point, Cenarion Hold, Light's Hope, Booty Bay, Moonglade, Nighthaven, Powderfuse Port) have one Alliance and one Horde flight point under the same name.
- **Two wrong flight points:**
  - The Alliance Moonglade steps named the druid-only "Nighthaven, Moonglade" at Sindrayl; it is now "Moonglade", his public flight point.
  - Aerie Peak's flight path pointed at Talonaxe instead of Guthrum Thunderfist.
- **Sara Balloo's Plea and A King's Tribute (683, 686)** were accepted twice. The 30-40 route runs the whole chain, including 689, so they are removed from the Wetlands 26-30 Ironforge stop.
- **MacKreel's Moonshine (647)** was only handed in by the Southshore alternate, so the main route carried it into the 50s (log 21 of 20). The 30-40 route's Southshore inn stop now takes it (optional).

After the fixes: "Route audit passed (12229 steps)". Against main, the only new warnings are the three level notes above, and three old distance warnings are gone.

## Open decisions for the owner

- **Quest log headroom (Alliance).** The Westfall Deadmines pickups are normal steps, but their turn-ins are dungeon-only, so the route audit counts them in the log for the rest of the route. That leaves the first Wetlands coast trip at 20 of 20. Making those pickups dungeon-only would free six slots, but changes Westfall for players who skip the dungeon.
- **Troll Charm (6462, Horde)** fits the Thistlefur trip, but only if `camp-taurajo` frees a log slot (it would reach 21 of 20). The optional chest and turn-in steps are already in place.
- **Report to Ironforge (301)** is registered without its prerequisite (298), so catch-up doesn't send a player who skipped Ironband back to the dig. Its step comes right after the 298 hand-in.
- **Class quests no route step uses** (for the class files): Hunter's Path (6071-6076, 6721, 6722); the Priest quests Returning Home, Desperate Prayer, Divine Grace and Confounding Flash; the Druid's Heeding the Call (5924/5925) and 27; the Paladin tomes; the Dwarf Shaman's 97263 and 96243; the Mage's 1919; the Rogue's Second-Story Work (77573, Zygor only); and a Warlock-only Manhunt (147).
