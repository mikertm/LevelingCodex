# Alliance route 40-50 and the Riverglades: loops, gaps, carried chains, elites and shopping (2026-11-14)

Second pass over the Alliance 40-50 chapters in `QuestData4050.lua` and the two Riverglades alternates in
`QuestData3040.lua` (first passes: `2026-11-10-alliance-40-50.md`, `2026-11-10-alliance-30-40.md` for the
Riverglades, `2026-11-12-horde-30-60-integration.md`). Same brief as the Horde pass
(`2026-11-14-horde-40-50.md`): loops, hand-offs, gaps, vendor and Auction House items, optional and dungeon
chains, and the new elite convention. Main chapters: `desolace-40`, `badlands-41`, `stv-41`, `tanaris-43`,
`feralas-44`, `hinterlands-48`, `ungoro-49`. Alternates: `riverglades-farholde`, `riverglades-shroud`,
`shendralas`, `searing-gorge`, `blasted-lands-47`. Every chapter stays unverified until it is played.

## Left out, and why

- Ongeku, Khan Jehn, Khan Hratha and the Valley of Bones chain (1373, 1374, 1380, 93034-93128, and "A Cleansing
  Cry" which Wowhead lists between 93041 and 93045 but the old data lacked): need Ongeku (above).
- Rumormonger to Report Back to Fizzlebub (1115-1122): 1115 would sit from 43 to 48 for a swamp quest the route
  never reaches again.
- Safety First (1189): a third Shimmering Flats trip. Zamek's Distraction (1191), A Bad Egg (2750), Pristine Yeti
  Hide (7735), 349: not in the client.
- Safety Checks (97617, Dorgaron, Badlands 54.6, 12.9): where the helmet and mattock lie is published only in the
