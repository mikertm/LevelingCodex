# Alliance alternate route expansion

The seven chapters give Alliance players more ways to reach level 30 from the existing racial routes. Their level bands are suggested starting and return points. A character short of the next band can finish another chapter before coming back. All seven are marked unverified until a live run checks travel, quest availability and experience.

| Chapter | Levels | Route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ashenvale: Astranaar and the Coast | 18–24 | Darkshore and Maestra's Post, Astranaar, Teronis and the Zoram Strand, then Bathran's Haunt and Stardust |
| Stonetalon Mountains | 22–28 | Astranaar through Talondeep Path, the Windshear Crag circuit, Pridewings and Stonetalon Peak, with Charred Vale around 25 |
| Ashenvale: Raynewood and Forest Song | 24–30 | Silverwind and the Foulweald camp, Raynewood and the Howling Vale, Forest Song and Felfire Hill, then the last Raene hand-ins |
| Wetlands: The Green Belt and Coast | 20–25 | Dun Algaz, Mosshide camps, Menethil, Bluegill coast and nearby crocolisks |
| Lakeshire: Shadowhide and Blackrock | 23–29 | Lakeshire, Fangore's Shadowhide camp and the Blackrock caves |
| Darkshire: Worgen and the Night Watch | 25–30 | Darkshire and the worgen woods, plus Tranquil Gardens and Raven Hill for the Watch |
| Southshore: Coastal Patrols | 27–30 | Ferry and flight path, Farren's western murlocs, and an eastern naga follow-up around 30 |

## Source decisions

- The committed Forever client reference, build `1.60.1.70862`, contains every routed quest ID, map and named flight path. The expansion covers 62 distinct quest IDs, including the IDs shared with the existing Wetlands, Darkshore and Duskwood routes. The cached QuestieDB Forever tables provide the quest text, links and NPC points. Zygor's local Forever 1.6.37551 guide provided a second check on town stops, quest order and entrances. The Codex route instructions were written independently. [Forever client data](https://wago.tools/db2/QuestV2?build=1.60.1.70862) · [Pinned QuestieDB Forever data](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/tree/0f7d8adc92b28ac6594f4ad8745257f4d9784edf/data/Forever)
- Balthule's letter (967) is now available in the Darkshore chapter after 966. The early Ashenvale chapter can also fetch it from scratch, then closes the local Delgren circuit with 970 and 973. The coast pairs the statuette with Wrathtail heads, and Raene's first two steps carry over by quest ID to the late chapter. Bathran's Hair opens at 20. [Ashenvale guide](https://wowforeverinfo.com/guides/ashenvale-alliance-18-30/) · [Ashenvale quest and travel map](https://bindonforever.com/zones/ashenvale)
- Stonetalon pairs the Venture Co. kills with the blueprint drop, and pairs the Pridewing venom with Forever's Stonetalon Supply Run (86574). The tunnel markers are on both sides of Talondeep Path. Journey to Stonetalon Peak (1056) is a breadcrumb for the Charred Vale hunt. Sources differ on the number of harpies of each type, so the objective names let the live log supply the counts. [Stonetalon zone guide](https://bindonforever.com/zones/stonetalon-mountains) · [Stonetalon map](https://wowfdb.com/zones/stonetalon-mountains/map/) · [Journey to Stonetalon Peak](https://www.60.tools/quests/1056-journey-to-stonetalon-peak)
- The late Ashenvale route uses the Shrine of Aessina spawn for Sentinel Melyria because the Forever quest text mentions Forest Song while recorded positions put her near the shrine. It uses the observed pendant position and quest level for Gleaming Crescent Necklace (98027), with the level 25 pickup from the base records pending a live check. The final Raene trip provides the transformation item on Krolg's dialogue steps. Mage Summoner takes the player into the Barrens and sits outside this chapter. [The Howling Vale](https://bindonforever.com/quest/1022) · [Gleaming Crescent Necklace](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98027/gleaming-crescent-necklace) · [Krolg and Ran Bloodtooth](https://bindonforever.com/quest/1045) · [Mage Summoner](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/1017/)
- The early Wetlands section uses the same Greenwarden, timber and Bluegill quest IDs as the level 26–30 route. The Menethil crocolisk loop uses the observed six-skin objective. Forever records disagree about Halloran's chain order, so the step gives a manual exit when Young Crocolisk Skins is unavailable. The route leaves the tougher Green Belt and excavation quests for the later chapter. [Wetlands route](https://bindonforever.com/zones/wetlands) · [Young Crocolisk Skins](https://azeroth.wiki/forever/quests/young-crocolisk-skins) · [Halloran quest chain reports](https://forever-tavern.com/dungeons/excavation-site-wetlands)
- Late Lakeshire covers the Shadowhide pendants, Fangore, Blackrock Champions and Keeshan's escort. Late Darkshire shares the first two Night Watch IDs with the backbone so players who already did them can start the Plague Spreader assignment when ready. The new Redridge and Duskwood loops are opt-in choices for a fresh route. [Redridge zone](https://bindonforever.com/zones/redridge-mountains) · [Duskwood zone](https://bindonforever.com/zones/duskwood)
- Southshore starts with Darla's flight path. Farren's western murlocs are level 30–32, so the first combat leg targets about 29 with a partner or 30 solo. Farren's Proof reuses the same coast before the level 30 check for eastern naga. The navigation uses the northern Menethil pier, the west Auberdine pier and the dock south of Southshore. Southshore's `TaxiNodes` marker identifies the ferry on the client map, while the boat path source supplies the boarding waypoint farther down the pier. ## Live checks

- Leave Darkshore with Balthule's letter and enter Ashenvale on foot. Run the west-coast loop at 18–20, record the level at the Bathran and Stonetalon checks, and verify the older Darkshore and Raene rows clear if the quests are already done.
- Cross Talondeep Path in both directions. Confirm the courier pickup, the bundles and venom dropping on the same Pridewings, and Albagorm's kill counts. Check the Astranaar and Stonetalon flight fallback on a character with both paths and one with only a single path.
- In late Ashenvale, confirm Melyria's location, the pendant pickup level, the Wooden Key chest, the Rotting Slime chests and the transformation prompts at Krolg. Record the level before Ran Bloodtooth and after the final hand-ins.
- Enter the Wetlands from Loch Modan, then check Halloran's first-visit availability and how many coastal crocolisks spawn. Switch to the later Wetlands chapter and confirm its reused hand-ins are already complete. Confirm the Redridge and Duskwood late choices remain available without replacing the main route on a fresh login.
- Board at Auberdine and Menethil on separate trips. Check the Southshore stop, Darla's flight path and the return trip toward each continent. Compare solo and paired levels for the first two Farren quests.
