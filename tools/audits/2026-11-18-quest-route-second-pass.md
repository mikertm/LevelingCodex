# Alliance and Horde quest routes, second pass

This pass starts at `7dd34b8` and carries forward the 326-chapter inventory, source history, and live checks. Comparison of the loaded guides shows changes in 14 Alliance chapters and 15 Horde chapters. [First-pass coverage and zone inventory](2026-11-18-quest-route-coverage.md)

## Method

Compared pickup and turn-in names and pins, starter items, faction masks, minimums, and prerequisite IDs with the client snapshot and the current QuestieDB Forever base and player traces. Each cross-chapter pickup was then traced to its delivery or cleanup. The first scan counted 143 Alliance and 211 Horde pickup occurrences that close outside their own chapter. Used the Nov 10 supplied RestedXP and Zygor guides to check routes and player-facing names, and checked the reported discrepancies against Forever quest records and prior live observations. A mismatch in a static source was a lead for review. ## Record decisions

- **Priest.** The records and current published quest data list Brother Joshua as the pickup for 5676 and Laurena as the recipient. Zygor takes it directly from Laurena in the same room, so the step names both. Zygor and Forever reports agree on Rohan's local Chastise interaction. Some inherited records list a Laurena referral for 5641. RestedXP confirms Miles Welsh's 5644 pickup for an Undead Priest even though QDB points to Zayus. Verify the exact offer variants on the intended priest races. - **Class and dungeon flags.** The 4967/4968 faction masks disagree with the actual Orgrimmar and Stormwind trainers and a published Forever route. Keep the city-appropriate Orb of Orahil chains. The prior Aquatic Form ID findings, Afadra's Old Ironforge approach, and the documented level-24 offer for 299 still take precedence over the corresponding database flags.
