# First Aid 1–300 audit

The route uses WoW: Forever client build `1.60.1.70862`. The game lists First Aid as skill line 129, with 2942 as its child line. The reference contains 32 named recipes. Spell IDs, reagents, icons and skill-up cutoffs come from the client. The source audit also checks trainer availability, manuals and quest requirements against the Forever guides and QuestieDB. ## Progression

Stop crafting when you reach the next skill target. The shopping list estimates 153 Linen Cloth, 147 Wool Cloth, 126 Silk Cloth, 92 Mageweave Cloth and 64 Runecloth from skill 1. ## Reference B and Forever cross-check

- Reference B and the Forever trainer guides agree on the rank caps and the ten bandage recipes. Train Journeyman at skill 50 before passing 75. Dedicated First Aid trainer lists give no separate character-level requirement for Journeyman. Some general guides list level 10, so training below level 10 is worth checking in game. Silk Bandage is taught at skill 150. [Forever rank table](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/first-aid-trainers) · [Silk Bandage trainer record](https://foreverdb.net/item/6450)
- Read **Expert First Aid - Under Wraps** at 125. The client item 16084 has no character-level requirement. The two bandage manuals require 180 and 210. Deneb Walker sells all three in Stromgarde Keep for Alliance, and Balai Lok'Wein sells them in Brackenwall Village for Horde. The client marks all three items tradable. [Expert book record](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=16084/expert-first-aid-under-wraps) · [Forever manual vendors](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/first-aid)
- At **225 First Aid and character level 35**, complete Triage with Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen in Theramore (Alliance, quest 6624) or Doctor Gregory Victor in Hammerfall (Horde, quest 6622). Save 15 patients before six die using the doctor's bandages. Reference B's Horde acceptance line agrees with the Forever quest record. Later lines in the reference B Horde guide refer to the Alliance ID. The same doctors are the listed sources for the later bandage recipes at 240, 260 and 290. [Alliance Triage](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6624/triage) · [Horde Triage](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6622/triage) · [Forever First Aid overview](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/first-aid/overview)
- Forever moved all six standard healing potions to First Aid. The add-on lists Minor Healing Potion and Healing Potion as optional leveling crafts and verifies their reagents and cutoffs against client spells 1244431 and 1244433. Regular Runecloth Bandage can also finish the route through green skill-ups if the player skips the Heavy Runecloth recipe. [Potion learn skills](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/first-aid/overview#first-aid-recipes-in-forever) · [Runecloth route](https://www.60.tools/guides/first-aid-leveling-guide)
- The camp section lists First Aid Kit at 20, Toxin Study at 140 from Commander Springvale in Shadowfang Keep, and Plague Doctor's Laboratory at 300. The Laboratory's blueprint item and effect are confirmed. Its drop source still needs confirmation.
