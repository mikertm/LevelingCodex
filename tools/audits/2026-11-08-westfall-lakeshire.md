# Westfall and Lakeshire route audit

The Human start now hands off at the Furlbrow farm. Westfall covers levels 12-18 and Lakeshire covers 17-22 for Alliance races. A level 19 Skyborne begins in Lakeshire. At level 17 or 18 the current zone decides between the overlapping sections. Both sections remain unverified pending a live run.

## Westfall

Polestar starts the farm errands and Sentinel Hill circuit around 11-15. Bind on Forever goes back and forth across several level bands for the flight paths, the farm loops and the Defias chain. The Codex batches the north shore with the gills and early Defias, the farms with harvesters and stew, and Moonbrook with the later Defias and industrial supplies. The required Wiley visit opens the Lakeshire flight path. The first Stormwind trip combines the Shaw and Sprite handoffs and the dungeon pickups. [Polestar Westfall](https://polestar.guide/guides/alliance/02-westfall), [Bind on Forever Westfall](https://bindonforever.com/zones/westfall), [Judgement's Alliance guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/alliance-leveling-guide/).

Toxic Soil uses client questline 6026 and the QuestV2 IDs 92742, 92744, 92745, 92747-92753 and 92819. Testing the Wells and Murloc Gills can run together and both unlock The State of the Mines. The route uses the client POIs for the wells and the two mine areas. Sprite's dynamite handoff can be skipped when supplies are unavailable. The separate Harvesting the Harvesters pickup is 92909. [Testing the Wells](https://bindonforever.com/quest/92742), [The State of the Mines](https://bindonforever.com/quest/92745), [Explosive Consultation](https://bindonforever.com/quest/92748), [Harvesting the Harvesters](https://bindonforever.com/quest/92909).

The main route includes the solo Defias chain through the escort and the Deadmines pickups in Sentinel Hill and Stormwind. The dungeon switch exposes the run, its objectives and turn-ins, and the detonator finale. Red Silk Bandanas and the two Thistlenettle quests have work in the mine outside the portal, grouped with the dungeon visit for this route. Check the timing of the messenger and escort with the recorder. [Forever Deadmines quest planning](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/dungeons/deadmines/quests/), [Bind on Forever Deadmines](https://bindonforever.com/dungeons/deadmines).

## Lakeshire

The route starts with the local gnolls and Lake Everstill. It batches the shoes and Stormwind messenger, then pairs Everstill Bridge with the north-side gnolls and whelps. Show of Force (98407), Alther's Mill (98386) and Blackrock Blockade (98387) come from Forever records. Alther's Mill follows Everstill Bridge. Blackrock Blockade shares the southeast trip with Blackrock Menace. Stonewatch elites and deeper chain follow-ups are candidates for the later 20-30 milestone. [Bind on Forever Redridge](https://bindonforever.com/zones/redridge-mountains), [Questing.wiki Redridge](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/leveling/redridge/), [Show of Force](https://bindonforever.com/quest/98407), [Alther's Mill](https://bindonforever.com/quest/98386), [Blackrock Blockade](https://bindonforever.com/quest/98387).

Loch Modan and Darkshore offer outdoor XP between early Westfall and the tougher Moonbrook and Redridge quests. The first shared route stays in the eastern human zones. Record actual levels after the farm loop, before the Defias escort, and at the Lakeshire handoff to see how much XP is available without a dungeon.
