# Fishing 1–300 audit

WoW: Forever build `1.60.1.70862` uses Fishing skill line 356 and child line 2943. The guide follows base Fishing skill for ranks. Lures improve effective skill for catching fish. Successful catches advance the skill even in starter waters, and later points take more catches. [Client skill line](https://wago.tools/db2/SkillLine?build=1.60.1.70862) · [Forever fishing progression](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/fishing-leveling-guide)

## Progression

| Base skill | Where to fish | Rank or lure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–75 | Starter waters for your faction, including Zephras Isle for Skyborne | Learn Apprentice from a trainer. A Shiny Bauble gives +25 while you get started. |
| 75–150 | Stay in starter waters or fish in a capital | Train Journeyman at Fishing 50 and character level 10. Nightcrawlers give +50 from Fishing 50 if needed. |
| 150–225 | Booty Bay docks or Nat Pagle's island in Tidefury Cove | Read Expert Fishing - The Bass and You at Fishing 125 and character level 20. Bright Baubles give +75 from Fishing 100. |
| 225–300 | Nat Pagle's island in Tidefury Cove | Finish the Artisan quest at Fishing 225 and character level 35. Fish at the island without a lure or use a lure for Feralas and Tanaris until your effective skill reaches 300. |

The client's item records confirm the book's skill and character level, and that it is tradable. The three lures last ten minutes. ## Reference guide and Forever cross-check

- A reference guide uses Arnold Leland in Stormwind and Lumak in Orgrimmar. The add-on also lists Forever's Zephras Isle trainers. It starts Apprentice at level 5, while WoW-Professions and Icy Veins list no character-level minimum for Forever. The guide uses level 1 for Apprentice and the documented level 10 and skill 50 for Journeyman. [Forever trainer ranks](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/fishing-trainers)
- Both of its faction routes buy the Expert book from Old Man Heming on the lower boardwalk in Booty Bay and fish from the docks to 225. The add-on also offers Tidefury Cove, where a Bright Bauble brings 150 base skill to 225 effective skill. The reference guide finishes in the Hinterlands. The add-on uses Icy Veins' Tidefury Cove finish, which saves a trip after turning in the Artisan quest. [Forever routes and effective skill](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/fishing-leveling-guide) · [Forever water requirements](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/fishing/overview-leveling#fishing-requirements-by-zone-in-forever)

The Forever QuestieDB record for quest 6607 lists Nat Pagle as both giver and turn-in, character level 35, Fishing 225, and no prerequisite quest. Accept it at Nat's island before catching the four quest fish in open water.
