# Forever Alliance Druid and Shaman class quests

QuestClassData.lua covers Night Elf Bear Form, Cure Poison, Aquatic Form and Cat Form for both Alliance Druid races, and all four Dwarf Shaman totems. Skyborne Bear Form stays on the main Zephras Isle route.

## Eligibility and evidence

- Blizzard's race matrix lists Night Elf and High Order Skyborne as Alliance Druids and Dwarf as the sole Alliance Shaman. Windshaper Skyborne Shaman chains belong to the Horde. [Blizzard race/class matrix](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24304075/)
- All added IDs are in `tools/reference/forever.json` and the cached `1.60.1.70862/QuestV2.csv`. QuestV2 proves the IDs exist. For quest details I used `questfacts.py` against the pinned Forever QuestieDB base and trace records, including the prior `scratchpad/ds` scans and saved quest pages. For the new Dwarf quests, those traces supply endpoints and objective text but omit many server-side class, race, minimum level and prerequisite fields. The Dwarf tags come from the official playable combinations and the Alliance Shaman quest evidence. The authored links follow the public walkthroughs. [QuestieDB revision](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/tree/0f7d8adc92b28ac6594f4ad8745257f4d9784edf) · [Dwarf totem walkthrough](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/dwarf-shaman-totem-quests/)

## Druid

- **Bear Form, Night Elf, level 10.** `5921 > 5929 > 5931 > 6001`. The side guide begins at Mathrengyl, where the existing inline `5923` from Kal ends. `5921` has no required predecessor in the pinned facts, so the guide also works from Mathrengyl directly and mentions handing in the breadcrumb if held. The guide covers the Great Bear Spirit, Silva's Druid flight, the Auberdine boat, the Moonkin Stone cave and speaking to Lunaclaw's spirit after the kill. Skyborne Bear Form already runs inline on Zephras as `94006 > 94638`. Other Heeding the Call trainer breadcrumbs were left out. [Moonglade quest](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=5921/moonglade) · [Body and Heart](https://bindonforever.com/quest/6001) · [Blizzard's Moondust fix](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-november-2/2360696) · [Silva's flight](https://wowforever.gg/en/atlas/knowledge/places/nighthaven-lp-nighthaven/)
- **Aquatic Form, Night Elf and High Order Skyborne, level 16.** `26 > 29 > 272 > 5061`. Start and end at Mathrengyl. The guide covers the timed Lake Elune'ara bauble, the Darkshore Agility and Westfall Endurance lockboxes, and joining the halves at the Shrine of Remulos. The opener is **26** in Bind on Forever, the Wowhead Forever quest record and Efficient's linked 70450 chain. The pinned QuestieDB assigns Mathrengyl and the Alliance Trial of the Lake prerequisite to **27**, and its other entries keep the old Night Elf-only mask. An earlier 70124 capture also calls 27 Alliance. The draft follows the later captures, which identify 27 as Horde and show both Alliance races on the shared chain. Efficient's generated objective helper shows Horde lockbox coordinates even on 272. The draft uses the Alliance lockboxes from the pinned object records and the Classic faction-specific locations corroborated by Forever references. [Alliance opener](https://bindonforever.com/quest/26) · [Wowhead Forever opener](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=26/a-lesson-to-learn) · [70450 race eligibility and chain](https://efficient.games/wowf/quests/id5061) · [Earlier conflicting capture](https://wowforever.ninja/quests/27-a-lesson-to-learn/) · [Lake trial](https://bindonforever.com/quest/29) · [Alliance lockboxes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Trial_of_the_Sea_Lion)
- **Gaps.** The Skyborne Stormwind Moonglade introduction `94914` and its Dalaran lead-in `94912` exist. The Aquatic side guide points Skyborne without the teleport to Sheldras in Stormwind. The inline Skyborne Bear quests now use minimum level 10. [Skyborne teleport introduction](https://urgametips.com/wow-forever/quest-guides/moonglade-sheldras-dendrite-high-order-skyborne/) · [Teleport: Moonglade](https://bindonforever.com/spell/18960) · [Ursera Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94006/the-great-ursera-spirit) · [Strength and Mercy](https://moredots.io/quest/94638)

## Shaman

- **Earth Totem, Dwarf, level 4 or catch-up.** `94373 > 94374 > 94375`. Kept as a side guide so a level 10+ character can return to Anvilmar without altering the Coldridge route. `96243` is a catch-up introduction from Ingrid or Eldrun. The first step accounts for players who already took it, and starts the actual trial directly at Teo. The quest text calls item 264356 a Frostmane Bear Pendant, while the cached client item name is **Iceclaw Bear Pendant**. The QuestieDB manifestation is a reused NPC with Horde spawn pins. The draft uses the Dun Morogh Spirit Stone. [Earth Sapta and manifestation](https://bindonforever.com/quest/94374) · [Pendant and drops](https://azeroth.wiki/forever/quests/call-of-earth-94373)
- **Fire Totem, Dwarf, available at 10.** `94449 > 94465 > 94466 > 94467 > 94468`. The opener pins Ingrid and names Teo and Eldrun as alternatives for the same ID. The reagents come from Tunnel Rat Geomancers and Stonesplinter Seers in Loch Modan. The shared reagent item records list Horde drops, and the shared brazier object points to Durotar. The Dwarf quest text and walkthrough put the ritual above Braldir at about `31.8, 64.5` in Loch Modan. [Dwarf reagents and level](https://bindonforever.com/quest/94466) · [Dwarf shrine and brazier](https://bindonforever.com/quest/94467) · [Trainer alternatives and paths](https://forg.gg/en/forever/tips/shaman-totems)
- **Water Totem, Dwarf, available at 20.** `94494 > 94495 > 94497 > 94499 > 94500 > 94501 > 94502 > 94503 > 94505`. Each waterskin has its own collection and turn-in. The final ritual runs from Norric to the Forgotten Shrine and the two manifestations at Stendel's Pond in Westfall, then back to Norric. Some generated endpoints place the shared manifestation or bracer drop in Silverpine. The quest text and walkthrough put the Dwarf ritual in Westfall. The Shrine pin is from the cache. The manifestation pin is approximate. ForG puts the red waterskin at `73.7, 61.6`, a Wowhead player report gives `74.9, 63.0`, and Bind on Forever pins `66.3, 62.9`. The draft pins the eastern falls and uses the Nightcrawler Murlocs as the landmark. [Water chain IDs](https://theforeverguides.com/quests/shaman-call-of-water/) · [Brown waterskin](https://bindonforever.com/quest/94497) · [Red waterskin alternate pin](https://bindonforever.com/quest/94499) · [Red waterskin player report](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94499/call-of-water) · [Dwarf ritual](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94502/call-of-water) · [Boat and sample route](https://forg.gg/en/forever/tips/shaman-totems)
- **Gaps.** The separate Earth, Fire and Water Sapta recovery records `94472`, `94473` and `94616` appear in the cached facts but are absent from the client quest ID list. They were omitted. The Dwarf starter rune is already inline.

## In-game checks

- At level 14, Cure Poison starts with Lessons Anew (6121) and runs through Power over Poison (6125). The same Alliance IDs are documented for Night Elf and High Order Skyborne. At level 20, Cat Form starts at 98393. The spirit branches use 98394/98396/98731 for Night Elf and 98341/98404/98738 for Skyborne before both return to Darnassus for 98397. Confirm the Skyborne branch and the statue that grants stealth in the Barrow Dens. [Forever Cure Poison route](https://polestar.guide/guides/class/druid/cure-poison) · [Forever Cat Form route](https://polestar.guide/guides/class/druid/cat-form)
- At level 30, check that Dwarf Call of Air (94789) sends you from Eldrun in Ironforge to Veyric Thunderhame in Arathi Highlands and awards the Air Totem. The optional follow-up chain is outside this guide. [Air Totem quest record](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/quests/94789)

- On a level 10 Night Elf, verify Mathrengyl offers `5921` after the inline hand-in and also when approached directly. Check that using the Moondust, defeating Lunaclaw and talking to the spirit advances `6001`.
- On a level 16 Night Elf and High Order Skyborne, confirm Mathrengyl offers `26`, that Dendrite offers `29` after it, and that the two Alliance lockboxes and `5061` work on both races. Verify the Skyborne teleport source if the spell is missing.
- On a Dwarf, confirm the Earth spirit and Fire brazier waypoints, and that the Fire opener is offered by the listed trainers. For Water, verify the exact Redridge fill point, the Stendel's Pond spirit location and the handoff from the shrine to `94503` and `94505`.
