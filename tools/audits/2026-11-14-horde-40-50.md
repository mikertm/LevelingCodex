# Horde route 40-50 and the Riverglades: loops, gaps, dungeon chains and shopping (2026-11-14)

Second pass over the Horde 40-50 chapters in `QuestHorde4050.lua` and the Riverglades and Shen'dralas
alternates in `QuestHordeRiverglades.lua` (first passes: `2026-11-12-horde-40-50.md`,
`2026-11-12-horde-riverglades.md`, `2026-11-12-horde-30-60-integration.md`). The owner asked for the Horde
1-60 loops to be polished together, smartly looped, with the gaps, optional quests, dungeon chains and vendor
or Auction House items for quests covered. Main chapters: `stv-40`, `swamp-41`, `desolace-41`, `tanaris-41`,
`feralas-43`, `stv-44`, `swamp-45`, `tanaris-46`, `hinterlands-48`, `feralas-49`. Alternates: `dustwallow-40`,
`searing-45`, `blasted-47`, `ungoro-48`, `riverglades-rogmar`, `riverglades-bolderok`, `riverglades-eastsea`,
`shendralas-pylons`, and the Horde `dungeon-krol-dok-stronghold` guide. Every chapter stays unverified until it
is played.

## Sources

- Quest facts: the QuestieDB Forever cache (commit 0f7d8ad) through `tools/questfacts.py` (givers, enders,
  levels, `requiredLevel`, prerequisites, item drops, NPC and object spawns) and the cache's NPC table (ranks:
  `QuestDangerData.lua` is generated from it). Every new coordinate is a QuestieDB spawn or cluster centre
  unless the step carries an `uncertain` note.
