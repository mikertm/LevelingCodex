# Cooking 1–300 audit

This route uses WoW: Forever client build `1.60.1.70862` and Cooking skill line 185. Spell IDs, reagents, icons, skill-up cutoffs and crafting stations come from the client. Every step uses a Cooking Fire. [Client recipe thresholds](https://wago.tools/db2/SkillLineAbility?build=1.60.1.70862) · [Client reagents](https://wago.tools/db2/SpellReagents?build=1.60.1.70862) · [Client station requirements](https://wago.tools/db2/SpellCastingRequirements?build=1.60.1.70862)

## Progression

Stop crafting when you reach the next skill target. The shopping list rounds up expected crafts, and actual counts vary once a recipe turns yellow. It reserves the Artisan quest ingredients until the cap rises to 300.

## Reference B and Forever cross-check

- Both reference B faction guides use a trainer for the first two ranks, the Expert Cookbook for 225, and Dirge's quest for 300. Forever's trainer records also list Boiled Clams at 50, Dry Pork Ribs at 80 and Goblin Deviled Clams at 125. The guide uses level 1 for Apprentice and level 10 for Journeyman. Sources differ on whether Journeyman is available earlier, and the client tables lack that trainer requirement. [Forever recipe trainer](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/npcs/3191/training/) · [Level 10 rank table](https://www.60.tools/guides/cooking-leveling-guide) · [Alternate rank table](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/cooking-leveling/)
- Read the **Expert Cookbook** from skill 125. The Forever item has no character-level requirement and is tradable. Shandrina at Silverwind Refuge in Ashenvale sells it to Alliance. Wulan in Shadowprey Village in Desolace sells it to Horde. [Expert Cookbook](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=16072/expert-cookbook) · [Forever vendor guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/cooking-leveling-guide)
- Both reference B routes buy **Recipe: Roast Raptor** at 175. The add-on lists Hammon Karwn at Refuge Pointe and Keena at Hammerfall so either faction can buy it in Arathi Highlands. Reference B's Horde route uses Nerrist in Stranglethorn Vale. [Roast Raptor item](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=12228/recipe-roast-raptor) · [Forever vendor guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/cooking-leveling-guide)
- At Cooking **225** and character level **35**, bring **12 Giant Egg**, **10 Zesty Clam Meat** and **20 Alterac Swiss** to Dirge Quikcleave in the Gadgetzan inn in Tanaris. Reference B, the Forever QuestieDB record and the Forever quest guide agree on Clamlette Surprise (quest 6610). Buy **Recipe: Tender Wolf Steak** from Dirge on the same visit. [Clamlette Surprise](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6610/clamlette-surprise) · [Dirge's inventory](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/npcs/8125/?inventory=18046)
- Reference B's standalone Cooking routes finish with Poached Sunscale Salmon. Forever's client assigns both it and Baked Salmon to the Iron Oven. The add-on uses Nightfin Soup, which reference B's combined Cooking and Fishing routes buy from Gikkix in Tanaris. Nightfin Soup still uses a Cooking Fire and stays yellow through 300. Players with spare Tender Wolf Meat can keep cooking the steak through green skill-ups. [Gikkix's inventory](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/npcs/8137/?inventory=13945) · [Iron Oven blueprint](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=273115/blueprint-iron-oven)
- **Basic Campfire** uses Forever's kit recipe, spell 1229737. It is learned with Cooking and takes Simple Wood plus Flint and Tinder. Journeyman and Expert kit recipes unlock at 90 and 200, and their items require 140 and 220 to place. Cookie's Feast and the Iron Oven are separate camp features. The guide names the blueprint items for Expert Campfire and Iron Oven where their acquisition remains unconfirmed. [Blizzard camping overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301516/gather-around-the-campfire-in-world-of-warcraft-forever) · [Basic Campfire recipe](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1229737/basic-campfire) · [Forever Cooking and camp recipes](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/cooking)
