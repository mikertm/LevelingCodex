# Alliance 50-60: loops, gaps, elites, dungeon links and shopping (2026-11-14)

The owner asked for the whole Alliance route to be checked: chapters that connect and loop sensibly, missing quests,
optional and dungeon chains, elite quests, and the vendor or Auction House items quests need. This file covers levels
50-60 (`QuestData5060.lua`); 1-30, 30-40 and 40-50 were audited at the same time in their own files. The first pass for
this band is `2026-11-10-alliance-50-60.md`; its decisions stand unless this file changes them. Every section is still
`verified = false`.

## Sources

- **Quest facts**: quest database Forever at revision 0f7d8ad, read through `tools/questfacts.py` and small scripts on the
  same loader: names, levels, minimum levels, givers, enders, prerequisites, objectives, item drops, NPC and object
  spawns and ranks. Quest IDs and flight points checked against the client reference `tools/reference/forever.json`.
- **Reference guide A**: the reference guide A Forever Alliance chapters "49-50 Tanaris/Un'Goro" to "59-60 Winterspring/Silithus part
  2" (`local guide reference`), for quest IDs, grouping, order, where items are bought, and the positions of the three
  Talrendis Point quests (world coordinates converted with the client's Azshara extents; Commander Truesong comes out at
  11.78, 78.30, beside Loh'atu). No text was taken.
- **reference guide B Forever guides** (quest-ID gap check only).
- **Wowhead Forever** for Slay the Spitelash (88807), Home on the Range (89001) and Gettin' Buzzed (89013): objectives and
  the level-48 experience calculator link (the pages state no level).
- **Tools**: `tools/questloops.py ALLIANCE` (five races, nine classes), `tools/questroute.py audit`, and a quest-log
  simulation built on the audit's own rules (dungeons and optional steps on and off).
- The relay with the 30-40 and 40-50 auditors and the coordinator (Stranglethorn Fever, Super Sticky, Expedition
  Salvation, the Lar'korwi quests, Crystals of Power, Overseer Maltorius, the Riverglades and Uldaman clean-up notes).
