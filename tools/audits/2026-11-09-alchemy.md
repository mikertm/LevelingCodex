# Alchemy 1–300 audit

The route uses WoW: Forever client build `1.60.1.70862`. The client lists Alchemy as skill line 171 and awards recipe skill-ups to its child line 2937. The reference stores 195 recipe entries from the parent line. The harness compares the guide's spell IDs, thresholds, reagents and Stonescale Oil yield against those records. Trainer requirements and vendor stock are server data. Both starter recipes have `AcquireMethod=1`. The Alliance and Horde Zygor 1.6.37551 routes still start with standard Minor Healing Potions and include later standard healing potions. The corresponding six current client crafting spells are on First Aid (line 129, spells 1244431–1244436). The separate Discolored Healing Potion line remains in Alchemy. The Codex route avoids its faction quest chains. Minor Mana Potion uses Mageroyal and Peacebloom, plus an Empty Vial, and carries the guide through the first training cap. The last five points are green. Elixir of Minor Fortitude is the optional 65–90 path for players with Earthroot. The recipe has a deprecated teaching item in the client, and current Forever guides list it at trainers from skill 50. [Minor Mana Potion record](https://www.foreverdb.app/en/forever/items/2455/minor-mana-potion) · [Forever trainer recipe guide](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/alchemy-in-wow-forever-leveling-guide-and-trainers)

Train Journeyman at skill 50 and character level 10, Expert at 125 and level 20, and Artisan at 200 and level 35. Zygor's two faction guides agree on Ainethil and Doctor Herbert Halsey for Expert, and Kylanna Windwhisper and Rogvar for Artisan. The Forever trainer lists add the neutral Master Mycologist Shurome in Emerald Sanctuary, Felwood. Zygor also confirms Lesser Mana Potion and Elixir of Agility at the faction Expert trainers, and spell 11460 and Elixir of Greater Agility at the faction Artisan trainers. Spell 11460 is now Draught of Detect Undead. The final spell is **Elixir of Greater Defense** (17554). Its teaching item retains **Recipe: Elixir of Superior Defense** (13478). Soolie Berryfizz in Ironforge and Kor'geld in Orgrimmar each carry the recipe with a one-copy stock limit. Each elixir takes two Stonescale Oil, one Sungrass and one Crystal Vial. Learn Stonescale Oil at 250. One craft yields two oil from one Stonescale Eel and one Leaded Vial. From 265 the add-on budgets 43 eels, 43 Leaded Vials, 43 Sungrass and 43 Crystal Vials. Superior Mana Potion is an optional 265–300 route if the defense recipe is out of stock. Its recipe can be learned at 260, and the client colors are 275 / 295 / 315. Zygor's Alliance and Horde routes agree on the alternate recipe vendors, Ulthir and Algernon. ## Camp objects

Mana Well unlocks at 20 through an Alchemy trainer or Camping 101. Fermenter uses the 140 blueprint from Rath'mael in the Ruins of Lordaeron. The Alchemy Laboratory blueprint requires skill 300. Its exact drop source is unconfirmed. None of these recipes is needed for the leveling route.
