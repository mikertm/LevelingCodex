# Forever profession guide audit, November 20, 2026

Reviewed all 12 guides and their 151 route steps. The client check used build **1.60.1.71037**, the latest listed for `wowf` on November 20. The profession audit returned zero errors against that build and the repository's 1.60.1.70888 snapshot. The newer tables changed no primary leveling recipe, guided smelt, route component, or rod. They incorporate changes the addon already described, including Tanning Rack at 125 and the Runed Golden Rod requirement for Arcane Salvager. The changed Tailoring bag costs apply to recipes outside the route. The shared reference remains on 1.60.1.70888 pending a full game-data refresh. [Client skill table](https://wago.tools/db2/SkillLineAbility?build=1.60.1.71037) · [Blizzard hotfixes through November 19](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-19-2026)

Used the written Wowhead Forever routes as the baseline and cross-checked route alternatives against WoW-Professions and the November 18 Forever Era guide. Recipes can differ between routes because of materials, travel, and vendor access. Kept the existing route when it passed the game checks and the other guide gave a valid alternative.
