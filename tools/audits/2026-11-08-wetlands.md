# Wetlands (26-30) route audit

The Wetlands section takes the route from the end of Duskwood (about 27) to level 30. It's unverified until it has been played with the recorder. All 27 quest IDs are in Forever's QuestV2 (build 1.60.1.70862). The facts come from `tools/questfacts.py`:
- givers, turn-ins, positions, levels and chains (QuestieDB Forever data)
- objective spots, from the `npc` / `item` spawn lookups

The step text is our own.

## Why the Wetlands

- **Forever sources.** They put Duskwood at 18-30, the Wetlands at 20-30 and Redridge at 15-25. Forever reworked the Wetlands, adding about 30 new quests at 22-34, and Forever's beta stopped at 30. [Mobalytics level ranges](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/guides/zone-map-level-ranges), [Warcraft Tavern Forever leveling](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/leveling), [Bind on Forever Wetlands](https://bindonforever.com/zones/wetlands).
- **XP.** A classic quest only loses XP once you're more than five levels above it. So the zone's 22-26 quests still give full XP when you arrive at 27, and the new Forever quests (27-30) carry the rest.
- **Compared with Judgement's classic guide.** It reaches 30 by bouncing between Ashenvale, the Wetlands and Duskwood. Staying on one continent keeps the route on the eastern side. [Judgement's Alliance guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/alliance-leveling-guide/).

## Order

- **Getting there.** Fly from Darkshire to Stormwind, take the Deeprun Tram, and learn the Ironforge and Thelsamar flight paths on the way north through Dun Algaz. The tram has no waypoint: the player presses Next.
  - A player who reaches the Wetlands another way skips these lead-in steps. The guide skips any section's travel steps once you're in that section's zone.
- **The Green Belt (about 27):**
  - Howin's Razormaw quests (Forever).
  - Rethiel's Tramping Paws and Fire Taboo, at the Mosshide camps to the south-east.
  - Raptor Ridge for the eggs and incisors together. Ground eggs and Highland or Elder Razormaw loot share the ridge.
  - The Greenwarden (463) is skipped. It only sends you to Rethiel, and Tramping Paws has no prerequisite.
- **West to Menethil (about 28):**
  - Pick up Uncovering the Past, then do Ormer's first raptors west of the site. Save the fragments for the second visit.
  - Fen Creepers for Blisters on the Land.
  - In Menethil Harbor: the flight path and the bind, then the harbor hub.
  - The coast: Spoils of War, the murlocs, the statuette crates, and the cursed wreck. Return the Statuette continues into Forever's 98189/98190, which ends with Bolvar in Stormwind.
- **Back east (about 29):**
  - Ormer's second raptor quest shares the dig-site sweep with Uncovering the Past. Follow it with Sarltooth.
  - Loot War Banners while sweeping four separate attack plans for From the Ashes (98221). Route order is Ironforge, Menethil Harbor, Southshore and Thelsamar. That is objective order 4, 1, 3, 2 in the client. The local Zygor reference and Forever objective map agree on the four areas. [Attack plan locations](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/98221/) · [Recorded objective pins](https://foreverdb.net/quest/98221-from-the-ashes)
  - Sida's ooze bag, and the Thelgen Rock gland (Forever).
  - The Fen Creepers turn-in at Rethiel.
- **The finish.** Hearth to Menethil for the turn-ins, fly to Ironforge and train if level 30, then take the tram to Bolvar. For Blisters on the Land (275), use the active quest-log count because sources list both eight and twelve Fen Creepers. [Eight in the Forever cache](https://theforeverera.com/es/quests/275/) · [Twelve in Questing.wiki](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/275/)

Sarltooth (29) and the Angerfang Dragonmaw (26-29) are normal mobs in both the classic and the Forever data, as in classic. So War Banners and From the Ashes (98221, Angerfang) are in the route.

## Taken from Bind on Forever's route, not the quest data

Forever's new quest items aren't in the QuestieDB data, so these spots come from Bind on Forever's Wetlands route:
- Spoils of War: harbor floor, 12.1, 52.5.
- Razormaw eggs: 68.3, 33.0.
- Razormaw incisors: 67.4, 30.8.
- Stalker gland: Thelgen Rock, 48.7, 62.4.

The required levels of the Forever quests aren't in the data. The route uses 20-22 (the classic quests around them) until the recorder confirms them.

## Left out

- **Young Crocolisk Skins and Apprentice's Duties (484/471):** low XP at 27 and a long detour.
- **Crocs of the Sky (98072) and Highland Hides (98815):** their drop sources aren't in the data.
- **Bloom of the Heavens (98208/98209):** it ends in Auberdine.
- **Lifting the Curse (290):** Captain Halyndor.
- **Death to the Dragonmaw (98291):** its targets are Forever's new Dragonmaw Retreat mobs (Reclaimer, Soulbinder, Infiltrator), levels 29-30 and elite in the Forever data.
- **Dun Modr and the Thandol Span chains:** level 30 and over.
- **Shaman-only:** Call of Water.
