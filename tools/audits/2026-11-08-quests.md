# Human 1-12 quest route audit

Client reference: WoW Forever 1.60.1.70862 from [wago.tools](https://wago.tools/db2/QuestV2?build=1.60.1.70862). QuestV2 verifies IDs. The UiMapAssignment rectangles produce the map widths and heights used by the arrow. QuestLineXQuest and the quest POI points confirm that Toxic Soil starts in Westfall. [Testing the Wells](https://bindonforever.com/quest/92742) identifies Alba Fairmoon as the giver, with two farm wells in the objectives. Carry this chain forward to the Westfall milestone.

Use `python -I tools/questfacts.py quest ID` with Lupa in the Python environment. Questie's live addon repo delegates entity storage to [QuestieDB](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/tree/0f7d8adc92b28ac6594f4ad8745257f4d9784edf). Its Classic and Forever source files and Forever player traces are cached locally. The route contains individual facts and original instructions. The full databases stay in the ignored cache. [ONN's quest catalog](https://www.oraclenewsnetwork.com/quests) supplies independently recorded quest entries and labels its Classic fallback.

## Northshire Valley

Judgement leaves after the Defias and Milly loop at about level 4. The Forever routes use the Echo Ridge sequence to get closer to level 5 or 6 and to unlock both the book and camping chains. The Codex pairs the vermin and wolves early, does Workers with the stolen books, and takes Mining Consultant on the way to the deeper mine. Brotherhood precedes Milly because the Forever route records that prerequisite. The old Questie base names Wolves Across the Border instead. [Judgement's Classic route](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/alliance-leveling-guide/), [ForG Elwynn](https://forg.gg/en/forever/zones/elwynn-forest), [Bind on Forever Elwynn](https://bindonforever.com/zones/elwynn-forest), [Milly Osworth](https://bindonforever.com/quest/3903).

Forever additions included here are Nibbled-On Book (91741), Book Inventory (92124), Rascally Rodents (91743), Mining Consultant (91745), The Big Picture (91752), Follow That Kobold! (91758), Shhh! We're Hunting Kobolds (91772), The Adventurer (96627) and the Human Hunter's A Scribbled Letter (92479). The observed trainer and route establish that 92479 belongs to Hunters. Warriors keep Simple Letter (3100). Some QuestieDB metadata disagrees with the published Human playthrough on eligibility, so check these offers with the recorder. [Book Inventory](https://bindonforever.com/quest/92124), [The Big Picture](https://bindonforever.com/quest/91752), [The Adventurer](https://bindonforever.com/quest/96627), [A Scribbled Letter](https://bindonforever.com/quest/92479).

## Elwynn Forest

Icy Veins recommends reaching 11 or 12 before leaving. Judgement leaves sooner and uses other Alliance zones, and ForG groups the farm quests and Fargodeep as separate visits. The Codex starts the mine pickups in Goldshire and combines their objectives with Goldtooth and Book Return on the same Fargodeep visit. Collecting Kelp shares the Crystal Lake murloc trip with the new quests. Jasperlode and Eastvale form one eastern loop. The Stormwind trip combines Shipment to Stormwind with the flight path pickup. The western sweep finishes at level 12 and carries Report to Gryan Stoutmantle into Westfall. [Icy Veins Classic route](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-classic/leveling-guide), [ForG Elwynn](https://forg.gg/en/forever/zones/elwynn-forest), [Bind on Forever Elwynn](https://bindonforever.com/zones/elwynn-forest), [Warcraft Tavern Forever leveling overview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/leveling).

Forever additions included here are The Great Outdoors (95998), Camping 101: Cooking (96626), Book Return (91775), Rare Books (91777), A Net Disaster (99127), Slimy Menace (99128), A Man About a Murloc (99129), Bottles and Baubles (99143) and Downstream (91733). Sam's fire is near the road, so the route adds Camping without a separate trip. Rare Books points back to Northshire on the way to Stormwind. Nimsy, Geosculptor Yip and Hogger can require help. Skipping their quest steps keeps the main route playable. [The Great Outdoors](https://bindonforever.com/quest/95998), [Camping 101: Cooking](https://bindonforever.com/quest/96626), [Rare Books](https://bindonforever.com/quest/91777), [Slimy Menace](https://bindonforever.com/quest/99128), [Downstream](https://bindonforever.com/quest/91733).

Both sections are unverified pending a Human playthrough. Record book drops, quest availability and chain gates, rare and elite targets, actual XP at Goldshire and at the Westfall handoff, and any travel pins that land off the road. Check the Tracking Kit and six tracks on the path from the stables to Goldshire. [ForeverWisp's Human guide](https://www.foreverwisp.com/guides/wow-forever-human-leveling-guide), [Kobold tracking quest](https://bindonforever.com/quest/91772).

Later passes can add optional class and profession detours and extend the Crystal Lake follow-up. ForeverQuest describes a tracker and map guidance. The Codex keeps its own route and progress model. No AllyRoute Forever listing was confirmed. [ForeverQuest listing](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/foreverquest/files/all).
