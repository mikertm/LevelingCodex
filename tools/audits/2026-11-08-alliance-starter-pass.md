# Alliance starter route completion pass

The four racial starts lead into Westfall. The route keeps a grind fallback where XP needs an in-game check. New quest IDs, map positions and the quest log cap are audited against the Forever 1.60.1.70862 reference and pinned QuestieDB cache.

## Elwynn and Westfall

- The first eastbound loop takes Delicate Instruments (91723) at the Tower of Azora and Good Steel (91732) in Eastvale before visiting Jasperlode. The second loop does Delicate Instruments (91724), Stolen Enchanting Supplies (91725), Cloth and Leather Armor (59), and Bounty on Murlocs (46), then turns in Good Steel. The armor and bounty unlock after Deliver Thomas' Report (39). Normal murlocs are the fin targets. [Tower chain](https://bindonforever.com/quest/91723) · [Supplies](https://bindonforever.com/quest/91725) · [Mining tools](https://bindonforever.com/quest/91732) · [Murloc bounty](https://bindonforever.com/quest/46) · [Armor hand-in](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=59/cloth-and-leather-armor)
- An Enticing Offer (99130) follows A Man About a Murloc. Gather duskweed and animal blood on the return to the farms for the potion and Princess turn-ins. Baited for Success (99131) ends at Jason on the way to the second eastern loop. [Bait materials and prerequisites](https://bindonforever.com/quest/99130) · [Jason's hand-in](https://bindonforever.com/quest/99131)
- The Collector (123) and Furlbrow's Deed (184) appear if the starter item is in bags or the quest is active. Gold Pickup Schedule (1307) is the starter, distinct from quest source item 2223. Westfall Deed (1972) is the starter, distinct from quest source item 1971. Manhunt is a separate encounter that can require help. [Collector starter](https://forg.gg/en/forever/quest/123) · [Westfall Deed](https://senseos.gg/wiki/wow-forever/items/1972/westfall-deed/) · [Furlbrow hand-in](https://bindonforever.com/quest/184)

## Coldridge and Dun Morogh

- The Forever quest records have no class mask for A Refugee's Quandary (3361), Scalding Mornbrew Delivery (3364) or Bring Back the Mug (3365). All Coldridge classes now take them after the troll whelps, with an uninterrupted run back to Durnan on the timer. The Adventurer (96628) follows Senir's Observations (282). The camping introduction stays on the route and the Cooking lesson that follows is a separate activity. [Forever route and class variants](https://bindonforever.com/zones/dun-morogh) · [Timed delivery](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/3364/)
- Tundra MacGrann's Stolen Stash (312) fits the west loop. Wait for Old Icebeard to patrol out of the cave before taking the meat. Underground Map (96391) fits the east loop and appears if the Dark Iron Map (274268) drops from a spy. Empty Powder Keg (268548) unlocks Stolen Blasting Powder (95213 then 95214). Both powder quests stay hidden without the keg or earlier quest progress. Shimmer Stout now leads to South Gate Outpost before the pilot chain at North Gate. The level-12 fallback is before training and the tram. [MacGrann](https://bindonforever.com/quest/312) · [Dark Iron Map](https://www.foreverdb.app/en/forever/items/274268/dark-iron-map) · [Keg](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=268548/empty-powder-keg) · [Thesten's follow-up](https://bindonforever.com/quest/95214)

## Shadowglen and Teldrassil

- Fang of Githyiss (97236) uses the same bag gate for item 277190. The Webwood Egg stop passes Githyiss's spawn so players can attempt the rare drop on the way. [Fang starter item](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=277190/fang-of-githyiss)
- Pick up Shayla's Bounty: Gnarlpine Furbolg (490) before the den and turn in with 15 fangs on the way toward Darnassus. After The Relics of Wakening (483), take Ursal the Mauler (486) on the south Teldrassil loop. Ursal is level 12 and the camp pull can bring adds. The route describes how to skip Ursal or Oakenscowl if the pull is unsafe. Recipe of the Kaldorei (4161) fits the final Dolanaar stop for characters with Cooking and seven Small Spider Legs already in their bags. [Blizzard's change to the bounty count](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-november-2/2360696) · [Bounty waypoint](https://forg.gg/en/forever/quest/490) · [Ursal](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/486/) · [Cooking quest](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/4161/)
- The route leaves Darnassus through the Temple Gardens portal, suggests the hippogryph from Rut'theran with the ferry as a fallback, then completes Return to Nessa (6343) before taking the south-pier ship from Auberdine to Stormwind. The installed reference B copy includes Return to Nessa in its general Night Elf route. The cached quest row records a Night Elf requirement and the section already applies that filter. If Laird has no follow-up in game, skip the pickup and continue from Auberdine. [Temple Gardens](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Temple_Gardens) · [Flight to Auberdine](https://bindonforever.com/quest/6342) · [Return to Nessa](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6343/return-to-nessa) · [Auberdine dock directions](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thamarian)

## High Order Skyborne

- The pinned Forever base and fixes give 95 corrected minimum levels plus prerequisites for the three Zephras hubs. The route adds the Mage's Glowing Recall Crystal and Fillion's bedroll interaction and locates Avenged Tenfold at the watchtower camp. [QuestieDB Forever reference](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/tree/0f7d8adc92b28ac6594f4ad8745257f4d9784edf/data/Forever) · [Zephras route objectives](https://polestar.guide/guides/alliance/skyborne/01-zephras-isle)
- Board at the eastern pier. Denaaris and the portal to Stormwind use Alterac Mountains UiMap 1416 in the cached data and reference B. Welcome to Azeroth (94947) ends at Bolvar, who offers Journey to Sentinel Hill (98021). Druids can take Child of Nature (94912) from Alfina to Sheldras on the way. [Eastern pier](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/zephras-isle-zone-overview) · [Denaaris and Bolvar](https://azeroth.wiki/forever/quests/welcome-to-azeroth) · [Druid referral](https://bindonforever.com/quest/94912) · [Journey to Sentinel Hill](https://pd.zroot.it/forever/quests/98021)

## Priest

- Added level-5 Garments of the Light guides for Humans (5623 then 5624) and Dwarves/Gnomes (5626 then 5625). The latter's cached Classic-era mask lists only Dwarf. Forever's records and the walkthrough also list Gnome. Both branches call for Lesser Heal (Rank 2) and Power Word: Fortitude, and award the robes. The Night Elf garment chain was already in Teldrassil. ## November 9 shared objectives pass

The published Forever client and the committed reference both report build 1.60.1.70862. The route check also used the Forever quest and spawn cache and Blizzard's November 6 hotfix notes. Those notes include the quarry trogg respawn adjustment, the shared Ammo for Rumbleshot interaction fix, the Teldrassil Lasher immunity fix, and the Lotheluum trainer fix. [Client quest table](https://wago.tools/db2/QuestV2?build=1.60.1.70862) · [Blizzard hotfixes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-6-2026/2403040)

- Northshire and Elwynn show shared progress for the Echo Ridge book drops, Fargodeep books, dust and candles, Crystal Lake murlocs, Eastvale wood and wildlife, Brackwell bandits and Princess, and Riverpaw armbands and Hogger. Jasperlode now combines exploration, geomancer kills, tools and rare books in a single visit. Reference B's route also groups Jasperlode and the Eastvale wildlife loop. [Elwynn quest records](https://bindonforever.com/zones/elwynn-forest) · [Good Steel](https://bindonforever.com/quest/91732)
- Coldridge shows the regular and burly trogg counts during the mail and boar steps and the journal and Felix items on the troll cave trip. Dun Morogh groups the ammo run with food and fur, the Grizzled Den quests in one clear, the quarry trogg hunts, and Dark Iron spies with nearby leopards. [Dun Morogh quest records](https://bindonforever.com/zones/dun-morogh) · [Flintfire shipment](https://bindonforever.com/quest/98321) · [Frosthowl](https://bindonforever.com/quest/98326)
- Shadowglen groups grell moss and ground totems. Teldrassil groups Lake Al'Ameth seeds and sprouts, Starbreeze objectives, and the Barrow Den relics, charm and fangs. Shayla's bounty is available before the ambusher kills, and its current requirement is 15 fangs. [Teldrassil quest records](https://bindonforever.com/zones/teldrassil) · [Ambusher drops](https://bindonforever.com/npc/2152) · [Shayla's bounty](https://forg.gg/en/forever/quest/490)
- Zephras groups windstones with the Thendal combat route, windstone drops with the bandit cave, feather pickups with hippogryph kills, and supplies with Falaath combat. Valanaar groups the orchard field objectives, watchtower cultists and charms, ruins chests and Wind Hollows, and Shadowgale ears, heirlooms, herbs, feathers and talons. [Zephras quest records](https://bindonforever.com/zones/zephras-isle) · [Avenged Tenfold](https://bindonforever.com/quest/92834) · [Brawler loot records](https://efficient.games/wowf/npcs/id270201)
