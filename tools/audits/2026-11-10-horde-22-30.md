# Horde 22-30: Silverpine, Hillsbrad, southern Barrens, Stonetalon, Ashenvale, Thousand Needles

`QuestHorde22to30.lua`. Backbone: the RestedXP Forever "Speedrun Guide 22-30" chapters in `forever/RestedXP-Skyborne.lua` (index 129 "22-24 Hillsbrad", 130 "24-26 Southern Barrens/Stonetalon", 131 "26-28 Ashenvale", 132 "28-30 Thousand Needles"), with the "Dungeon Guide 22-30" variants (162-168) for which pickups belong to dungeons and the classic `RestedXP Horde 20-30.lua` (including the "JJ" order) for comparison. RestedXP gave the quest grouping and order only; every step text is our own. Facts (IDs, levels, NPCs, positions, prerequisites) come from `tools/questfacts.py` (QuestieDB Forever 0f7d8ad); every quest ID and flight name is in `tools/reference/forever.json`. All sections are `verified = false`.

## Simplifications

- One route for every class. Dropped: hunter taming, the Westfall swim for "...and that note you found" (79007) and its Stonetalon follow-ups (79192 Stepping Stones, 79980 Scramble, 79974 Wet Job; this chain crosses five zones and ends in Loch Modan at 32+), the Brackenwall Village flight path detour, first aid and vendor stops, Isha Awak (873, an elite in Ratchet's waters) and its breadcrumb turn-in 874, the RFK, Gnomeregan and Shimmering Flats legs at the end of chapter 132.
- Class quests left out for a class-quest pass: Shaman Call of Water (1536 at the Tarren Mill well, 1534, 220, 63), Rogue Hinott's Assistance (2479/2480) and Deathstalkers in Shadowfang as a rogue pickup, Warrior Trial at the Field of Giants (1823/1824) and The Islander chain (1718/1719), Druid Aquatic Form (30/31), Undead Paladin weapon chain (Diplomatic Incident, A Curious Pair 91859 to Old Fire-Eye 95140, Lumina Windsinger, Fenris Isle; see https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-paladin-weapon-quest/), Undead Return to Podrig (6324).
- Trainer visits: Undercity at 24 (Warrior, Rogue, Priest, Mage, Warlock), Thunder Bluff at 24 (Hunter, Shaman, Druid, Paladin), Thunder Bluff at 28 and 30 (every Thunder Bluff class). Warlocks have no Thunder Bluff trainer: they train at 24 in the Undercity and at 28/30 in Orgrimmar on their own. Alodan the Hopeful and Boarton Shadetotem are missing from QuestieDB; their positions are RestedXP's.
- Hearth: Splintertree Post in Ashenvale, Freewind Post in Thousand Needles.

## Integration notes

- 441 Raleigh and the Undercity and 530 A Husband's Revenge are only offered to players who did The Engraved Ring in Silverpine. With the 440 chain restored, the engine cuts them for everyone else; until then the step text says to use Next.
- If the 12-22 route already does Arachnophobia 6284 or The Ashenvale Hunt 6382/6383 (RestedXP gives them to some classes before 22), drop ours.
- Sun Rock, Zoram'gar and the Crossroads flight paths are assumed known from 12-22; Undercity, Sepulcher, Tarren Mill, Splintertree and Freewind are learned here.
- Arrow legs added with `road()` (kept if an earlier file set them): Durotar/Orgrimmar <-> Tirisfal zeppelin, Tirisfal -> Silverpine, Barrens -> Ashenvale past Mor'shan Rampart, Stonetalon -> Ashenvale through Talondeep Path, Barrens -> Thousand Needles by the Great Lift (44.1, 91.4, from RestedXP's waypoint). The Tirisfal zeppelin tower position (60.8, 58.7) is approximate.
- Cross-file `suggest` wanted: the last 12-22 section should suggest `sepulcher`; a Stonetalon or Ashenvale 12-22 alternate could suggest `barrens-south` or `ashenvale-splintertree`.

## Alternate

`shimmering-flats` (29-31): Rocket Car Parts 1110, Salt Flat Venom 1104, Hardened Shells 1105, Load Lightening 1176 at the racing camp. Self-contained; offered by `thousand-needles` (`suggest`). RestedXP picks these up at the end of 28-30 and does them at 33-34; mobs are 30-34, so it is for players who reach 30 early. Goblin Sponsorship 1178 (Ratchet), A Bump in the Road 1175 (level 33 basilisks) and Wharfmaster Dizzywig 1111 are left to the 30-40 route. If the 30-40 builder routes the Flats, drop this alternate.
