# Horde 36-40 and Alliance 41-45 audit (2026-11-25)

Compared the Horde chapters stv-35, dustwallow-37, arathi-38, badlands-39, stv-40, swamp-41 and
desolace-41, and the Alliance chapters badlands-41, stv-41, tanaris-43 and feralas-44, with the Forever
Zygor guides (the owner's reference copy, comparison only) by quest ID, and ran `questloops.py` for both
factions. No new loop problems; the Stranglethorn back-and-forth is forced by the Nesingwary and Grom'gol
chains.

## Horde
- Added Some Assembly Required (577): Drizzlik gives it when Supply and Demand is handed in at the end of
  stv-35. The Snapjaw Crocolisks at Lake Nazferiti (40.8, 24.3, quest database spawns) are killed in stv-40
  after Bhag'thera, before the hearth to Grom'gol, and it is handed in on swamp-41's Booty Bay stop.
- Added Excelsior (628, elite Elder Saltwater Crocolisk, level 38): taken from Drizzlik on swamp-41's Booty
  Bay stop, the crocolisk killed in stv-44 just south-east of Grom'gol (33.4, 32.5; another spawn at 29.6,
  23.9) on the way to King Bangalash, handed in on stv-44's second Booty Bay visit. The elite question is
  asked at the pickup, like every elite quest.
- Not added: Frostmaw (1136, Melor Stonehoof in Thunder Bluff). It needs a trip to the Alterac Mountains,
  which the Horde route doesn't pass at that level.

## Alliance badlands-41
- Added Safety Checks (97617, Forever-only, Dorgaron at 54.62, 12.85 by the north road). Taken on the way in;
  the Explorers' League Mining Helmet is in the supplies beside the Crumpled Map (53.03, 34.65); the 8
  Unstudied Artifacts drop from the Stonevault troggs and the Pristine Mattock lies at 49.25, 68.22, both
  alongside Indurium. Handed in after the hearth to Thelsamar, on the way to Ironband (about 530 yd more
  riding). Level 40 / min 35 is an estimate: no published record gives it.
- To keep the log at 20 for warlocks, Briarthorn's Summon Felsteed letter (4487) is no longer picked up in
  Ironforge. It is only a breadcrumb: Strahad in Ratchet offers 4490 without it. The stv-41 hand-in of the
  letter is now optional.
- Not added: This Is Going to Be Hard (734/777/778). Zygor offers it only once Coolant Heads Prevail (713,
  Frost Oil) and Gyro... What? (714, a Gyrochronatom) are done; both need crafted items the route doesn't ask for.

## Alliance tanaris-43
- Not added: Safety First (1189, Shreev to Razzeric). The route reaches the Shimmering Flats before
  Gadgetzan's Safety First hand-in, so it would need a second ride to the Flats.
- Not added as a step: Zamek's Distraction (1191). It is a repeatable talk quest that sends Zamek off so
  Rizzle's plans can be taken; the Keeping Pace step already says to ask him.
