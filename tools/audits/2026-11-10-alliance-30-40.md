# Alliance route 30-40 (2026-11-10)

New file `QuestData3040.lua`. It also holds the shared 30-60 travel data (map sizes for every 30-60 zone, flight networks, boats and road legs) and the `B.section`, `B.on`, `B.note`, `B.optional` and `B.road` helpers that `QuestData4050.lua` and `QuestData5060.lua` use.

## Sources

- Order and grouping: two reference guides' Alliance 30-40 chapters (both copies of each chapter compared), read for quest IDs and order only. Every step text is written here.
- Quest facts (givers, enders, levels, races, prerequisites, item drops, spawn areas): the QuestieDB Forever cache via `tools/questfacts.py` (same commit as the 1-30 audits) and the Forever client reference (`tools/reference/forever.json`: quest IDs, map sizes, flight path names).
- Riverglades (Forever zone, no QuestieDB data): the Wowhead Forever quest pages (zone 16591 listing and each quest page's giver, ender, map points and series), read before Wowhead started showing a human-verification page; [warcraft.wiki.gg Riverglades](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Riverglades) and [Farholde Keep](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Farholde_Keep) for access, hubs and flight paths.
- Forever quests in classic zones (Stranglethorn 97047, 97048, 97328, 97329, 97331; Desolace 97284): names and objectives from the reference guides' quest IDs and objective lists; givers from the reference coordinates.

Breadcrumbs into the Riverglades are optional pickups (The Riverglades Report from Refuge Pointe is left out: the 30s quest log has no room for it): Deliver the Second Shipment (`swamp-38`), Lost Shipping Crate (`stv-36`). Their turn-ins in `riverglades-farholde` are optional.

Every level 30-41 has three or more leveling sections. Each alternate is in some section's `suggest`; no 30+ suggestion points at a section that ends before the suggesting one (the `riverglades-shroud` exits get the 40-50 sections at merge).

## Gaps and decisions

- **Quest log.** The 1-30 main route carries six Deadmines quests that a player who skips the dungeon never turns in; with them, Stranglethorn needs 26 slots. A `note` step can now carry `drop = { questIDs }` (quests the player abandons there); `tools/questroute.py audit` removes them from its simulated log. Two clean-up notes: Deadmines at the start of `darkshire-30`, Blackfathom Deeps and Excavation Site in Booty Bay. Dropped as low value for the slot they hold: A Daughter's Love (231), Supply and Demand (575/577), Assassin's Contract (522), Hemet Nesingwary breadcrumb (5762), Parts for Kravel (1112) and the Shimmering Flats follow-ups (1107, 1114-1118, 1183-1188).
- **Skipped as group or class content:** Colonel Kurzen (202), Troll Witchery (205), Mythology of the Titans (1050), Triage (6624, First Aid), Rhapsody's Kalimdor Kocktail (1452, Kalimdor items; for the 40s), Magical Analysis's follow-up (603), Sunken Treasure's Booty Bay hand-in (669), Ongeku (1370/1373). Class-filtered reference steps (Warrior Whirlwind chain 1718/1719/1791/1712/1713/1792, Warlock 4736/4738/1798/1758/4487/4965/4968/1799/4488/4490/4962, Paladin 1661, Shaman 94789/94502-94505, Druid trainer trips, Hunter 1107) are left for `QuestClassData.lua`.
- **Riverglades objectives** the quest pages don't spell out are marked `uncertain` (item names, sample spots, elite status). Quests with no usable objective text were left out: Hats Off (94923), Stuff 'Em and Stick 'Em (95156), The Sharpest of Blades (95184), Keeping the Light On (95613), Foreman's Plight (95943), High Stakes (96009), Not My Circus (95982), Gaseous Gold (96063), Rock 'em and Sock 'em (96067), Tessa Dawnbright's chain (95561, 95913, 95961, 95975), Proof of Profit (95934), Hail the Dragonslayer (95935), Upstream Contamination (96085), the Tanaris smuggling chain (95294-95472) and the Booty Bay contact chain (95295-95327).
- **Dungeon pickups** (`forDungeon`): Bring the Light (3636, Stormwind, Razorfen Downs), Eye of Mugg (94982), Twilight Mementos (94983) and Tome of Twilight (94984) for Krol'dok Stronghold. Krol'dok Moulding (94980) starts from a dungeon drop and is left for the dungeon guide.

## Loop check

A script walks each section's waypoints and flags a turn-in or available pickup the route passed within 40 yd of, walked more than 150 yd away from, and came back to. Fixed: Kravel's Ratchet errand, Brant's and Cornelius's pickups in the Riverglades, Trapclaw's turn-in order, the Shroud pickups at the start, Smuggler's Paradise, Vahlarriel's Vengeance and Krazek's Cookery taken on the first visit. Left: Highperch Venom (152 yd inside Theramore's town) and Singing Blue Shards (163 yd inside Booty Bay).
