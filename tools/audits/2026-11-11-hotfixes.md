# Forever hotfixes Nov 5-10, 2026: profession and route changes (build 1.60.1.70888)

Sources: Blizzard's "WoW: Forever Hotfixes" thread (Kaivax, last edited 11 Nov 03:27 UTC),
[forum thread](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2403040) ·
[news.blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026).
Client data: `python tools/gamedata.py update` moved the reference from 1.60.1.70862 to
1.60.1.70888 (wago.tools, 10 Nov). The only change in the new build is the Swiftmend tooltip
("...of the periodic effect." without "of the consumed spell"); `Data.lua` never quotes it. The
audit reports 0 errors.

None of the profession hotfixes below is in the client tables: Coarse Grinding Stone is still
yellow 50 / grey 75, the Arcane Salvager still names the Runed Truesilver Rod and the Tanning Rack
blueprint still says 140. They are server-side, so the guide text carries them and the reference
doesn't. If a later build carries them, `update` will show the diff.

## ProfessionData.lua changes

Mirror each into the profession's guide page.

- **Blacksmithing 65-80 (Copper Chain Belt), alt: Coarse Grinding Stone gives points to 90 (Nov 10)**
  - before: `"Runed Copper Gauntlets (8 Copper Bar, 2 Rough Grinding Stone)"`
  - after: `"Coarse Grinding Stone (2 Coarse Stone): Blizzard's November 10 hotfix lets it give points up to 90 (was 75), so it's a cheap filler if you mine Tin. Or Runed Copper Gauntlets (8 Copper Bar, 2 Rough Grinding Stone)"`
  - Why only an alternative: the notes give the new grey (90), not the new yellow. If the client's
    yellow (50) still holds, the stone is green from 70, so most of 65-90 would be green and
    the audit would warn. The crafting window shows the real colour. Classic taught it at 75; the
    Forever learn level isn't in the client, so the text doesn't give one.
- **Blacksmithing 80-95 (Runed Copper Belt), alt: same option up to 90**
  - before: `"Rough Bronze Leggings (6 Bronze Bar, orange until 120) if your trainer already teaches them"`
  - after: `"Rough Bronze Leggings (6 Bronze Bar, orange until 120) if your trainer already teaches them, or Coarse Grinding Stone (2 Coarse Stone) up to 90"`
- **Enchanting camp, Arcane Salvager: Runed Golden Rod, bonus for lower-level enchanters (Nov 10)**
  - before: `"Improves disenchanting at camp and provides the lute's buff."`
  - after: `"Crafting it needs a Runed Golden Rod, which the route makes at 155 (Blizzard changed it from the Truesilver rod on November 10). Improves disenchanting at camp, now for lower-level enchanters too, and provides the lute's buff."`
- **Cooking camp, Journeyman Campfire: placed from Cooking 90 (Nov 10)**
  - before: `"Craft the kit with Star Wood and Flint and Tinder. Place it from 140 Cooking to support up to five other camp features."`
  - after: `"Craft the kit with Star Wood and Flint and Tinder. Place it from 90 Cooking to support up to five other camp features. Blizzard lowered the placing requirement from 140 to 90 on November 10."`
- **Engineering 190-195 (Iron Grenade), note: vendor price 1s 25c (Nov 10)**
  - before: `"Use any Heavy Blasting Powder left from the sheep. Each grenade craft makes three."`
  - after: `"Use any Heavy Blasting Powder left from the sheep. Each grenade craft makes three. Use them in fights: since November 10 a vendor pays only 1s 25c each (was 5s)."`
- **Fishing camp, Fishing Rack: lure fix and open issue (Nov 10)**
  - before: `"Provides lures and lets you catch uncommon fish for one hour. It also provides the Fish Bowl benefit."`
  - after: `"... It also provides the Fish Bowl benefit. Blizzard fixed a bug that withheld its lures on November 10, but says some \"There is no loot\" errors remain."`

## Quest route changes

- **Zephras Isle, Camping 101: Herbalism (97968) finishes in Shen'dar Village (Nov 10)**
  - before: accept at Raan "... You finish it in Valanaar."; turn-in at Syriel Nightrain (57.9, 75.5)
    as the second step of `valanaar`.
  - after: accept text drops the Valanaar sentence; turn-in at **Halassa Fernbreeze**, the Shen'dar
    herbalism trainer (43.05, 43.52), between the Skinning (Mendalass) and Mining (Messana)
    turn-ins in `shendar`. The Valanaar step is gone.
  - Who: the notes only say "Shendar Village". Halassa is the village's herbalism trainer (both
    taught-by lists in `2026-11-08-herbalism.md`; WoW-Professions puts her at 43, 43.5). The
    Mining and Skinning lessons already end at the village's gathering trainers. QuestieDB
    (0f7d8ad) still names Syriel and gives Halassa seven points between 41.8 and 44.8, so the
    step uses the point nearest the trainer guide's coordinates. **Check in game.**
- **Razorfen Kraul prep, Lonebrow's Journal (1100): the journal moved (Nov 9)**
  - before: `"Loot and read Henrig Lonebrow's Journal."`
  - after: `"Loot and read Henrig Lonebrow's Journal. Blizzard moved it a little down the road on November 9: if it isn't here, look for a similar skeleton nearby."`
  - The notes don't give the new spot. Update `journal` (30.73, 24.35, Thousand Needles) once
    someone records it.
