# Alliance dungeon guides, levels 30-60 (2026-11-10)

`QuestDungeonData3060.lua` now holds a full guide (prepare / inside / after, bosses in order, sources,
`uncertain` notes) for every 30-60 dungeon; the two thin 1-30 guides (Scarlet Monastery: Graveyard and
Shadowfang Keep) in `QuestDungeonData.lua` were filled in. All guides stay `verified = false`.

## Sources

- **Quest facts** (IDs, names, levels, minimum levels, givers, enders, coordinates, prerequisites, item
  starters, objective items and the objects that hold them): the Forever QuestieDB records at revision
  0f7d8ad (`tools/questfacts.py` and a JSON dump of the same data). Every quest ID was checked against the
  client quest list in `tools/reference/forever.json` by `tools/questroute.py audit`.
- **Quest order and which quests belong to which dungeon**: the two reference guides' dungeon chapters
  (Scarlet Monastery to Dire Maul North), read for IDs and order only. All step text and boss tips are our
  own wording.
- **Krol'dok Stronghold** (no QuestieDB data, no reference chapter; wowhead shows a verification page):
  the Riverglades steps already in `QuestData3040.lua`, the client tables (`QuestV2` has 94926, 94980,
  94981, 94983, 94997, 94998; `ItemSparse`: Krol'dok Moulding 267138 starts 94980, Komuk's Warmaul 266889
  starts 94926), and two published guides: conquestcapped.com (entrance, bosses, keys, Alliance quests)
  and warcrafttavern.com. Everything not in the client data is marked `uncertain`.
- **Entrances**: classic map positions kept from the stubs (Forever uses the classic world maps), Krol'dok
  from the published guides (37.5, 37.3). Dire Maul wing doors, Uldaman and the two thin guides' doors are
  flagged for an in-game check.
- Instance IDs: as given (Scarlet Monastery 189, Razorfen Downs 129, Uldaman 70, Zul'Farrak 209, Maraudon
  349, Sunken Temple 109, Blackrock Depths 230, Blackrock Spire 229, Dire Maul 429, Scholomance 289,
  Stratholme 329). Krol'dok has no known ID and keeps the English-name fallback (`instance`), which
  `inDungeon` only uses when an instanceID is present: its enter step never auto-completes.

## Decisions

- **Lead-in chains live in the guides.** Each guide is audited on its own path, so every accept's
  prerequisite is handed in earlier inside the guide. Route steps are reused with `copy(section, ids)`
  (same quest metadata, `optional`/`dungeon`/`also` stripped), with `insertAfter` to slot a pickup next to the
  route step at the same NPC. For a player who followed the route those steps are already done.
- **Left out (long cross-guide chains or not in the client):** A Taste of Flame 4024 (needs the Searing
  Gorge chain 3441-3481 and 4022), Dark Iron Legacy 3801/3802, the Marshal Windsor / Onyxia chain from 4241,
  Attunement to the Core 7848, Seal of Ascension 4742/4743, the Scholomance key chain, Tinkee's egg chain
  4907/4734/4735 and Dawn's Gambit 4771/5531/5522, Hydraxis's Eye of the Emberseer 6821, the Mosh'aru
  tablets 5065/4788 and The God Hakkar 3528/4787 (need Zul'Farrak's 3527 handed in in another guide),
  Uldaman's Power Stones 2418 and The Lost Tablets of Will 762/1139, The Love Potion 4201, Healthy Dragon
  Scale 5582 (not in the client quest list), the Gordok Ogre Suit 5518/5519 and Free Knot 5525/7429,
  Vengeance of the Highborne (Krol'dok, no quest ID found).
- **Second runs.** Quests that need another visit after a hand-in (Moira 4362, Maxwell's Mission 5081,
  Above and Beyond 5263, The Active Agent 5213, Ras Frostwhisper 5466) sit in the after phase with "on your
  next run" in the text.
- **Elven Legends 7482** is placed in Dire Maul West (Kariel in the Athenaeum); one reference does it in the
  Gordok wing. Marked uncertain.
- **Overmaster Pyron 4262** is done before entering Blackrock Depths and handed in at Morgan's Vigil for
  Incendius!, following the reference order; some guides put Pyron inside. Marked uncertain.
- Treasure of the Shen'dralar uses the Alliance ID 7462 (7877 is the Horde version in QuestieDB).
- Krol'dok follow-ups 94997 "Lingering Essence" and 94998 "Curios of Curiosity" and the key quest 94981
  "Key Ingredients" use names from the published guide; the chain 94983 -> 94997 -> 94998 and 94980 ->
  94981 follow the IDs' order. All marked uncertain; the game shows its own quest names in the log.
