# Riverglades audit (2026-11-25)

Compared riverglades-farholde and riverglades-shroud (Alliance) with Zygor's Forever "Riverglades (38-40)"
guide (the owner's reference copy, used for comparison only) and wowforeverinfo.com, bindonforever.com and
wowforever.be. The quest records have no Riverglades data, and Wowhead's Forever quest pages came back empty.
Zygor has no Horde Riverglades guide. The Horde alternates (rogmar, bolderok, eastsea) were checked only
against the public guides' quest lists and the loop report, and were left as they are.

## Alliance riverglades-farholde
- Front half reordered to follow Zygor's single pass through the south-west. Freshwater Fiends (94614) now
  comes from Benjy Wheeler on the first pass. Zygor offers it before My Kingdom for a Horse, so the
  prerequisite 94613 was dropped. The Mudfin murlocs are visited once, for Halligan's heads, Benjy's kills
  and Robbie's hat. Halligan's plant food, saws and From Seed to Sprout are handed in before Farholde Keep
  instead of on a ride back west at the end of the chapter.
- Added Hats Off (94923, Robbie Holmsby at the Meadowsbrook farm, the hat from a Hollow Clam Shell at 49.3, 78.7).
- Brother Lowley's chain (96081-96084) is now optional. Zygor offers Wound Dressings only to a player holding
  20 Mageweave Cloth, and few have that at 36-38.
- Added The Sharpest of Blades (95184, Ember after Stuff 'Em and Stick 'Em, 8 Solid Sharpening Stones per
  Zygor) as optional.
- Added High Stakes (96009, Bertrand after Foreman's Plight). The 10 Wood Bundles come from Turner's
  Logging Camp on the chapter's last visit there, and the hand-in is at the start of riverglades-shroud
  (optional there, for players who skipped this chapter).
- Not added: Not My Circus (95982, 10 Briny Seafood Stew; no source says where the stew comes from),
  Stepped Out (95961; no source says where it leads), Lost Shipping Crate (94841; see the Desolace audit),
  Overseas Delivery (95144, from Tanaris).
- Unverified, to check in game: Freshwater Fiends without My Kingdom for a Horse, and the counts above.
