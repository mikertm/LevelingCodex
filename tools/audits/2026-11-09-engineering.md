# Engineering 1–300 audit

The route uses WoW: Forever client build `1.60.1.70862`. The reference contains 275 named records from Engineering skill line 202. Recipe spell IDs, reagents, yields used by later crafts, and yellow and gray cutoffs come from the client. Both tool slots come from `SpellTotems` and `TotemCategory`. Station names come from `SpellCastingRequirements` and `SpellFocusObject`. The anvil focus is ID 1. ## Reference guide and Forever cross-check

- Both faction routes of a reference guide agree on making an Arclight Spanner for six Copper Bars, training Expert with Springspindle Fizzlegear or Roxxik, and training Artisan with Buzzek Bracketswing in Gadgetzan. Forever trainer listings put Journeyman at skill 50 and level 10, Expert at 125 and level 20, and Artisan at 200 and level 35. [Forever trainer requirements](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/engineering-trainers)
- The reference guide still adjusts targets by 15 for Gnomes. Forever's Gnome racial reduces device failures and backfires, so the add-on uses the same skill targets for every race. The main route uses recipes available to either Engineering specialization. [Forever race and tool notes](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/engineering-leveling-guide)
- The route moves off Solid Blasting Powder at 190. It goes gray at 195, so the guide takes the last five points with trainer-taught Iron Grenade. Dense Blasting Powder has five green points at the end. Hi-Explosive Bomb remains yellow at 250 and can reach 260 if the player has spare parts. [Iron Grenade recipe](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=3962/iron-grenade) · [Forever explosive recipe records](https://www.60.tools/recipes/3962-iron-grenade)
- Both of its faction routes name the same Thorium Widget schematic vendors used here, Gearcutter Cogspinner in Ironforge and Sovik in Orgrimmar. It sends players to Xizzer Fizzbolt in Everlook for the Thorium Tube schematic. Forever vendor records also list Kor'gar in Riverglades. Client item 16047 binds on pickup and requires skill 275. The route uses Widgets through 285 while they are still yellow, then finishes with Tubes. The live vendors' stock may vary. [Widget vendor record](https://www.foreverdb.app/en/forever/items/16042/schematic-thorium-widget) · [Tube binding and vendor zones](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=16047/schematic-thorium-tube) · [Kor'gar's inventory](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/npcs/260565/?inventory=16047)
- Reagent Bot unlocks at 20 through a trainer or Camping 101. Sneed in The Deadmines drops the Repair Bot blueprint for skill 140. Anarchist's Workbench is a 300 blueprint. The exact Workbench drop source still needs confirmation. [Blizzard camping guide](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301516) · [Repair Bot blueprint listing](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/items/recipes/engineering) · [Workbench blueprint](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=273117/blueprint-anarchists-workbench)
