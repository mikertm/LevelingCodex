# Horde route 40-50 (2026-11-12)

New chapters in `QuestHorde4050.lua`: ten main-route sections from Grom'gol at 40 to the Ratchet boat at 50, and four alternates. Main chapters go through `H.chapter`, so a hand-in for a quest the 30-40 chapters give stays optional until that pickup is on the main route (the 30-40 file is written in parallel).

## Sources

- Order and grouping: reference guide A's Horde chapters 40-41 Stranglethorn Vale through 49-50 Feralas, and its dungeon copies (46-48 Tanaris, 48-48 Zul'Farrak, 49-50 Maraudon) for which outdoor pickups feed a dungeon; reference guide B's Horde 40-50 chapters (Stranglethorn and Swamp, Desolace, Tanaris, Dustwallow, Feralas, Hinterlands, Feralas and Un'Goro) for the Dustwallow-first order and the Searing Gorge and Un'Goro options. Read for quest IDs and order only; every step text is written here. Note for the lead: in the current copy of reference guide A the 40-50 chapters start about 2,090 lines later than the task's line ranges (40-41 Stranglethorn begins at line 101079).
- Quest facts (givers, enders, levels, races, prerequisites, item drops, spawn clusters): the QuestieDB Forever cache via `tools/questfacts.py` (commit 0f7d8ad). Every NPC and object spot is its QuestieDB spawn; objective spots are spawn-cluster centres. A few area waypoints reuse spots the Alliance 40-50 file already took from the same data (Sandsorrow Watch pool, the Valley of Bones spectres, the OOX escort ends, the Un'Goro ramp, Lar'korwi's mates).
- The Forever client reference `tools/reference/forever.json`: quest IDs, flight node names and links (Kargath-Grom'gol, Stonard-Booty Bay, Grom'gol-Stonard, Brackenwall-Orgrimmar, Gadgetzan-Camp Mojache), map extents (the Tanaris end of the Shimmering Flats road is the Thousand Needles crossing converted with them).
- Forever-only quests (no QuestieDB record): [Wowhead Forever 95773](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95773), [93430](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93430), [98192](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98192), [92878](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=92878), [93663](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93663); [conquestcapped: The Drowned City](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-the-drowned-city-guide/) for The Gurubashi Legends of Old (95748), Locating the Lost City (95770) and Enemies in the Water (95773): giver Kin'weelay, tablet at about 23, 12, Sunken Hollow at about 21.4, 27.8, Brinescale Skirmishers at about 22, 26. Their givers' spots come from QuestieDB (Kin'weelay, Commander Aggro'gosh, Krak, Blaise Montgomery, Dar, Magtoor).

## Gaps and decisions

- **Prerequisites fixed for the Horde:** the Alliance files register A Tale of Sorrow (2801) and The Stone Circle (3444) with the Alliance prerequisites (2783, 3445); QuestieDB accepts either, so this file re-registers them with 2623 and 3380 (`B.q`).
- **Left out to keep the log under 20 or save a trip:** A Threat in Feralas (2981, a pointer to Camp Mojache; its hand-in stays optional), Report Back to Fizzlebub (1122), Bone-Bladed Weapons (4300, level 52), Gammerita, Mon! (7816, needs Snapjaws turned in first), The Final Message to the Wildhammer (7843, a second trip to Aerie Peak), Consult Master Gadrin (2935).
- **Left for 50-60:** It's a Secret to Everybody part 3 (3908), The Apes of Un'Goro (4289), The Bait for Lar'korwi (4292), The Strength of Corruption (4120), Seeping Corruption (3568), Volcanic Activity (4502), Crystals of Power (4284).
- **Left to dungeon guides:** the Uldaman run quests and their after-chain (2202, 709, 728, 2342, 2278, 2280, 2440, 2965, 2966, 2954, 2967, 2968), the Maraudon run quests (7028, 7029, 7044, 7046, 7064, 7066, 7067), The Drowned City (95775, a Forever dungeon with no guide here).
- **No usable data:** Partial Mystical Notes (95384) and Torn Mystical Scroll (95385, item-started, hand-in NPC not in QuestieDB), the Booty Bay contact chain (95295, 95322), On the Other Side (95033). Not in the client: Zamek's Distraction (1191), the second Stranglethorn Fever (349), Perfect Yeti Hide (7738), the Egg-O-Matic eggs (2747-2750), the second-tier Bloodmage quests (2582, 2584, 2586, 2602, 2604). Alliance only: Suntara Stones (3367).
- **Forever quests kept with `uncertain` notes:** Lost in Transit (93663, the courier's spot), Taking the Bait Back (93430, Pelluk's spot and which fisherman), Skin Careful, Skin Sharp (98192), Lost for Words (92878, Namida's spot), The Gurubashi Legends of Old and Locating the Lost City (spots from a published guide).
- **Flight paths:** `getfp` steps at Grom'gol, Stonard, Booty Bay, Gadgetzan, Brackenwall, Camp Mojache, Revantusk, Marshal's Refuge and Thorium Point (auto-done when known). Flights to Gadgetzan, Brackenwall and Camp Mojache assume the 30s learned them; the step notes give the overland way.
- **Training:** Orgrimmar at 40; Thunder Bluff at 42 (all but warlocks); Thunder Bluff (druids, paladins) and Orgrimmar at 44; at 48 druids and paladins fly to Thunder Bluff on the way, everyone else trains in Orgrimmar. Riding at 40 is a note naming each race's trainer town.
- **Level warnings kept:** Whiskey Slim's Lost Grog (level 50) is picked up at 45 for the Hinterlands; The Swamp Talker (55, a level 50 elite, "use Next" note) and A Tale of Sorrow (57, a talk quest) at 45.
- **Loop check** (`tools/questloops.py HORDE ORC,TAUREN,SCOURGE`): fixed the Grom'gol pickups (taken before the Orgrimmar trip) and Dar's pickups (taken on arrival in Stonard). Left on purpose: the Booty Bay pickups at 44 (617, 621, 606, 348, 595, 580) and the Lost Rigger Cove pickups at 46 (2875, 8365) could be taken on the visits at 41, but holding them for three to five levels would push the log past 20.
- **Roads added:** Stranglethorn to Duskwood and the Swamp, Duskwood and Deadwind Pass to the Swamp, Tanaris to the Shimmering Flats.
