# Alliance alternate routes

Loch Modan (11-18) and Darkshore (12-20) are optional sections for every Alliance race. They use the existing route data model. Each branch has its own start and end. Dun Morogh offers Loch Modan before the tram, Teldrassil offers Darkshore in Auberdine before the Stormwind ship, and the Westfall and Lakeshire XP checks offer the branches. At the end of a branch the guide suggests an available shared section based on level and quest progress.

## Loch Modan

- Start at Thelsamar. Pair the first King's Lands troggs with The Trogg Threat in the Valley of Kings, then combine the Snowbound delivery with Algaz Station and Silver Stream Mine. Keep the remaining King's Lands quests on a later circuit. The last part has three linked enemies. Banner of the Fallen also has a skip for players who cannot handle the waves. [Forever Loch Modan records and route](https://bindonforever.com/zones/loch-modan)
- Gather idols and check the dig-site tools in the same visit. Item 278049 starts Excavation Tools (86613). The quest text names Magmar, while the client quest listing points to Prospector Ironband. They are at the same site, and the guide identifies both. [Excavation Tools quest](https://bindonforever.com/quest/86613) · [Starter item](https://pd.zroot.it/forever/items/278049)
- Finish Daryl's timed hunts before leaving Farstrider Lodge, then do the east shore, Bingles' supplies, and the Stonewrought Dam clue. Twisting the Knife (86758) appears for Dwarf, Gnome, Night Elf, and High Order Skyborne. It pairs with the normal crocolisk hunt, with an explicit skip if Daggerfang is absent. [Twisting the Knife eligibility](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=86758/twisting-the-knife)
- Shared progress now shows for Valley of Kings kills and teeth, Silver Stream Mine crates and ears, excavation tools and idols, and the two east-shore crocolisk quests. The tools step points to the ground pickup before the clear. The crocolisk turn-ins are before the northbound trip to Bingles. [Recorded excavation objective](https://foreverdb.net/quest/86613-excavation-tools)

## Darkshore

- Use Auberdine as home and work the nearest coast, Bashal'Aran, Ameth'Aran, the northern coast and Cliffspring, then the Grove of the Ancients and Master's Glaive. The final north loop covers Mathystra, the furbolg camps, and Gelkak. The route includes the first prospecting and Gyromast errands and leaves their tougher follow-ups for another visit. [Forever Darkshore records and route](https://bindonforever.com/zones/darkshore)
- Holy Diver (87760) follows all nine beached creature and turtle quests. Baron Marinous (98028) appears only with the Clouded Water Globe in bags or when already in the quest log. The southern Stormwind Harbor dock serves Auberdine. [Holy Diver](https://bindonforever.com/quest/87760) · [Baron Marinous](https://pd.zroot.it/forever/quests/98028) · [Business in Auberdine](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=97894/business-in-auberdine)
- The Ameth'Aran tablets, relics, Anaya and flame share one sweep. Mathystra relics share the naga clear. Moonstalker Sires and Matriarchs drop fangs and pelts. The cached spawn records place dual-drop cats near 60, 13 or in southern Darkshore, so the north-road fang step offers the far north as an option and keeps the southern pelt cleanup. [Moonstalker fang sources](https://azeroth.wiki/forever/quests/buzzbox-323) · [Moonstalker Sire](https://azeroth.wiki/forever/map/darkshore/moonstalker-sire)

## Live checks

- Record solo levels after the first Thelsamar circuit and before the late trogg and dam quests. Verify the Excavation Tools turn-in and the Snowbound timer.
- Record solo levels at the first Auberdine return and before Mathystra. Check the moonwell bowl, the nine beach turn-ins, and the Stormwind boarding point.
- At the Westfall XP check, try Loch Modan. Finish the branch and follow the suggested shared route. From that route, open Quests and resume an unfinished objective in another zone.
