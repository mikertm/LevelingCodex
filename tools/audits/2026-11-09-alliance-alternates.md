# Alliance alternate routes

Loch Modan (11-18) and Darkshore (12-20) are optional sections for every Alliance race. They use the existing route data model. Each branch has its own start and end. Dun Morogh offers Loch Modan before the tram, Teldrassil offers Darkshore in Auberdine before the Stormwind ship, and the Westfall and Lakeshire XP checks offer the branches. At the end of a branch the guide suggests an available shared section based on level and quest progress.

## Loch Modan

- Zygor's racial chapters pair the first King's Lands troggs with The Trogg Threat. Its Loch Modan 18–19 chapter pairs the two Marek hunts and the two dig-site gathers, then carries Excavation Progress Report to Thelsamar. Bind on Forever and Polestar support those batches. The Codex keeps the easier Valley of Kings pull and the mine in the opening circuit, then returns south for the harder troggs. Banner of the Fallen and the linked trio have skip instructions. [Bind on Forever Loch Modan](https://bindonforever.com/zones/loch-modan) · [Polestar Loch Modan](https://polestar.guide/guides/alliance/03-loch-modan)
- Pick up Thelsamar Blood Sausages before leaving town and work on its drops between hubs. Fill the Ceramic Jar after the south tower, then go straight to Norric. At Algaz Station pick up Filthy Paws, gather crates and rat ears in one mine visit, then turn the gear in before heading south. The roadside boars, bears, spiders and any remaining rat ears are cleanup on the way to Thelsamar. Sources differ on whether the filled jar still has a timer, so the route gives a direct delivery without specifying a duration. [Snowbound](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=86667/snowbound) · [Mine and northern circuit](https://bindonforever.com/zones/loch-modan)
- Gather idols and check tool piles on the same dig-site visit. Item 278049 starts Excavation Tools (86613). The quest text names Magmar, while the cached turn-in points to Prospector Ironband. The guide identifies both. Pick up Excavation Progress Report (298) at Ironband and carry it through the east-shore loop. Turn it in at Jern after the west-shore hand-ins. The next report and escort chain uses Ironforge and a return trip. [Excavation Tools](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=86613/excavation-tools) · [Excavation Progress Report](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=298/excavation-progress-report)
- Finish Daryl's timed hunts before the croc sweep. The first boar waypoint is the nearer cluster at 75.9, 45.3, with 65.1, 41.2 as a fallback. Work on Crocolisk Hunting and Twisting the Knife together after the timer, then turn both in to Marek. On the next northbound pass, Bingles has separate waypoints for the wrench, screwdriver, hammer and Blastencapper in quest-log order. The dam deliveries use distinct quest IDs, then the route follows the west shore toward Thelsamar. [Timed boar hunt](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=258/a-hunters-challenge) · [Bingles' Missing Supplies](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=2038/bingles-missing-supplies) · [Dam follow-up](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=199/a-dark-threat-looms)
- The audit checks all 23 routed quest IDs against the Forever reference. The viewer reads the client completion flag for ready turn-ins still in the log and uses the completion history for finished quests. A later turn-in moves the Thelsamar flight stop out of the active route during catch-up.

## Darkshore

- Use Auberdine as home and work the nearest coast, Bashal'Aran, Ameth'Aran, the northern coast and Cliffspring, then the Grove of the Ancients and Master's Glaive. The final north loop covers Mathystra, the furbolg camps, and Gelkak. The route includes the first prospecting and Gyromast errands and leaves their tougher follow-ups for another visit. [Forever Darkshore records and route](https://bindonforever.com/zones/darkshore)
- Holy Diver (87760) follows all nine beached creature and turtle quests. Baron Marinous (98028) appears only with the Clouded Water Globe in bags or when already in the quest log. The southern Stormwind Harbor dock serves Auberdine. [Holy Diver](https://bindonforever.com/quest/87760) · [Baron Marinous](https://pd.zroot.it/forever/quests/98028) · [Business in Auberdine](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=97894/business-in-auberdine)
- The Ameth'Aran tablets, relics, Anaya and flame share one sweep. Mathystra relics share the naga clear. Moonstalker Sires and Matriarchs drop fangs and pelts. The cached spawn records place dual-drop cats near 60, 13 or in southern Darkshore, so the north-road fang step offers the far north as an option and keeps the southern pelt cleanup. [Moonstalker fang sources](https://azeroth.wiki/forever/quests/buzzbox-323) · [Moonstalker Sire](https://azeroth.wiki/forever/map/darkshore/moonstalker-sire)

## Live checks

- Record solo levels after the first Thelsamar circuit and before the late trogg and dam quests. Check the Excavation Tools turn-in, whether Snowbound still shows a timer, and how often the nearer boar cluster supplies five kills. Confirm the live Bingles objective order advances the arrow at each tool. Reload on a character with prior Loch Modan turn-ins and confirm the guide bypasses the old flight stop.
- Record solo levels at the first Auberdine return and before Mathystra. Check the moonwell bowl, the nine beach turn-ins, and the Stormwind boarding point.
- At the Westfall XP check, try Loch Modan. Finish the branch and follow the suggested shared route. From that route, open Quests and resume an unfinished objective in another zone.
