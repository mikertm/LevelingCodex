# Alliance route 40-50 (QuestData4050.lua)

Date: 2026-11-10. Branch work for the 30-60 Alliance guide. Every route change lives in
`QuestData4050.lua`; nothing in `Data.lua` changed.

## Sources

- **Order and quest IDs:** a reference guide's Alliance 40-50 chapters (the speedrun set and its
  dungeon copy) and the second reference guide's 40-50 chapters, compared chapter by chapter. Facts
  and order only; every step text is our own.
- **Quest facts** (level, minimum level, givers, enders, coordinates, prerequisites, objectives,
  item drops): the Forever QuestieDB records (`tools/questfacts.py`, revision 0f7d8ad) and the
  client reference (`tools/reference/forever.json`: every quest ID exists in `QuestV2`, flight path
  names from `TaxiNodes`, map sizes from `UiMapAssignment`). Item IDs for Forever item-started
  quests come from the client's `ItemSparse` table.
- **Shen'dralas** (no QuestieDB data): wowhead.com/forever zone 16651 and its quest pages
  (giver, ender, map points, series), read once before the site started answering with a human
  verification page; warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shen'dralas for the setting. Sources are listed on the
  section.
- Zone pages on bindonforever.com and wowhead.com/forever for each section's `sources`.

## Decisions

- **Quests carried in from 30-40.** `carried()` in QuestData4050.lua: a turn-in or objective whose
  pickup is not on the leveling route before it becomes `optional` (shown while the quest is in
  the log), and a pickup whose prerequisite the route never offers is dropped. With the 30-40
  chapters in place this changes nothing. Assumed from the 30-40 chapters (reference order,
  Dustwallow2 path): 1373 Ongeku (from 1370), 1117 Rumors for Kravel, 554 Stormpike's Deciphering,
  653 Myzrael's Allies, 543 The Perenolde Tiara, 542 Return to Milton, 3636 Bring the Light,
  1116 Dream Dust in the Swamp, 669 Sunken Treasure, 603 Ansirem's Key, 196 Raptor Mastery,
  205 Troll Witchery, 193 Panther Mastery, 202 Colonel Kurzen, 1258 ... and Bugs,
  1187 Razzeric's Tweaking, 1452 Rhapsody's Kalimdor Kocktail in the log; prerequisites 188, 189,
  577, 605, 1421, 1458/1459 turned in.
- **Dungeon pickups stay off the route.** Tagging every outdoor pickup with `forDungeon` put the
  main route at 23-28 quests in the dungeon-planning path (cap 20), because those quests are only
  turned in inside the dungeon guides. They belong in each guide's prepare phase (list below).
- **Quest log.** The Southsea pirate quests wait for the second Tanaris visit, Lar'korwi's bait
  (4292, level 56) and The Stone Circle (3444) are left for the 50-60 Un'Goro chapter, and the
  Hinterlands chapter hands in the Booty Bay and Stormwind quests on its way back.
- **Left out:** 1459 (the 30-40 Desolace chapter does it, and 1466 needs it turned in first),
  1189 Safety First (a third Shimmering Flats trip), 6624 Triage (First Aid 225), the Green Hills
  of Stranglethorn (338 and chapters, page collecting), Lotwil's 713-716/734/777/778 and Pearl Diving
  705 (Auction House parts), 4284 Crystals of Power and 4289 The Apes of Un'Goro (50-60 Un'Goro),
  2741 egg chain (2747-2750 are not in the client), 1191 (not in the client), 95384 (no data),
  4450 Ledger from Tanaris and 3443/3452 (crafted daggers), the Bloodmages' second orders
  (2582/2584/2586/2602/2604 are not in the client).
- **Shen'dralas.** The Gelkis line in the Valley of Bones needs Ongeku (1373), so it sits in
  `desolace-40`. The alternate holds what has no prerequisite: the pylon chain (92914 to 93394),
  A Heart of Crystal / Mana Mania and three item-started quests (item IDs from ItemSparse).
  The Magram line (92743 onward) and the garden chain (93594 onward) have no published map points
  and are left out. 93128 Khan Jehn may repeat or replace classic 1374; flagged for a check.
