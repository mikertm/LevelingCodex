# Duskwood (21-27) route audit

The section follows Lakeshire and covers levels 21–27 in three loops out of Darkshire and a final visit to Abercrombie. It stays unverified until it has been played with the recorder. Quest IDs were checked against Forever's QuestV2 table for build 1.60.1.70862. `tools/questfacts.py` supplies the cached quest chain, NPC and item records. Spawn clusters from those records are starting points for the map arrow. The guide text is written for the Codex.

## Order

- Zygor's local 25–27 Duskwood and Redridge chapter gathers legs before accepting Dusky Crab Cakes. It pairs the first Night Watch with Skeleton Fingers, turns in both Smitts errands on one visit, and stacks the second Night Watch with Rot Blossoms. The November 8 Polestar route confirms the early western circuit. Bind on Forever also groups the cemetery drops and fits Ira's Dagger along the west road. Those batches fit the Codex's existing Lakeshire → Duskwood → Wetlands order. [Polestar 25–27](https://polestar.guide/guides/alliance/04-redridge-mountains) · [Bind on Forever Duskwood](https://bindonforever.com/zones/duskwood)
- **Loop 1** (about 21-23):
  - Bind in Darkshire.
  - Tranquil Gardens skeletons count for The Night Watch and drop Skeleton Fingers for the Totem of Infliction. Pick up Night Watch part two at Althea's first hand-in and save the Raven Hill clear for the Rot Blossom trip.
  - The optional Ira's Dagger pickup (96137) appears when starter item 281143 is in bags. The west road passes Lost Stalker spawns and ravagers near Yorgen Farmstead. The dagger goes back to Sirra on the same visit as the Raven Hill Tome. [Quest objectives](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96137/iras-dagger) · [Starter item and source](https://bindonforever.com/item/281143)
  - Go west from Dodds, pick up the tome before Jitters, and do the western spiders on the way to Sven's Camp. Keep six Gooey Spider Legs for Grual and collect venom for Eva from the same spiders. [Dusky Crab Cakes](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93/dusky-crab-cakes)
  - The dire wolves on the slopes toward the hermit (the spawn data puts them at 15-35, 24-27, between Lars and Abercrombie).
  - Back past the crossroads and Sven's farm mound to Darkshire.
  - The comb errand, with Black Widow Hatchlings north of Blind Mary's house (84, 53) for the crab cakes.
- **Loop 2** (about 24–25) goes to Sven and Lars together, then to Abercrombie. Back in town, visit Eva and Daltry before Smitts. Hand in Zombie Juice (158) and Inquire at the Inn (266) in the same stop so Gather Rot Blossoms (156) and Finding the Shadowy Figure (453) are both ready before leaving.
- **Loop 3** (about 25–27) takes the west road to Jitters for the crab cakes and Shadowy Figure. Clear Skeletal Fiends and Horrors once for Night Watch (57), Rot Blossoms (156) and remaining Skeleton Fingers for Totem of Infliction (101). Then finish Ghoul Fangs and turn in at Sven's camp before returning to Darkshire. Bring the juice to Abercrombie on the final visit and collect Ghoul Ribs. The route has travel waypoints for both long departures from Darkshire. [Rot Blossom prerequisites and drops](https://bindonforever.com/quest/156)
- **Wetlands handoff.** Below level 26, the guide offers remaining Duskwood quests or the Stockade as catch-up XP. The next travel waypoint takes players back to Darkshire for the flight. [Blizzard's dungeon XP hotfix](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dungeon-enemy-xp-increased-november-8/2406595/1)

## Left out for now

- **Seasoned Wolf Kabobs (90):** needs Stormwind Seasoning Herbs from Stormwind.
- **Look To The Stars** starts with a Bronze Tube. Guide sources list a vendor in Darkshire, with limited stock. The route leaves the chain for players who already have the tube or verify stock in game. [Look to the Stars](https://bindonforever.com/quest/174)
- **Messenger to Darkshire (145/146):** needs Messenger to Westfall (144), which the Westfall section doesn't take.
- **Later Duskwood** includes Proving Your Worth, Night Watch part three, Worgen in the Woods, Stalvan and the rest of the Embalmer chain. Proving Your Worth uses Raiders, Healers and Warders, a different set of mobs from the Rot Blossom sweep. Its follow-up Seeking Wisdom leads through Stormwind into Menethil and can pair with a later visit. [Proving Your Worth chain](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=323/proving-your-worth)
- **The Valor Family (96139)** is a Forever quest. Its required level (shown as 18) and its search spot in Raven Hill need the recorder.
