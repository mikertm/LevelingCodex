# Duskwood (21-27) route audit

The section follows Lakeshire: it is the first half of Duskwood, levels 21-27, done in three loops out of Darkshire. It stays unverified until it has been played with the recorder. Every quest ID is in the Forever client's QuestV2 table (build 1.60.1.70862). Givers, turn-ins, positions, levels and chains come from `tools/questfacts.py` (the QuestieDB Forever data). Objective spots come from the new `questfacts.py npc|item` lookups, which give the busiest spawn squares for each mob and drop. Step text is our own.

## Order

- **Sources.** Bind on Forever's Duskwood page has the dwarf/gnome order at 24-27, in five visits. Judgement's classic guide has three Duskwood visits (23-24, 26-27, 28-30) mixed with Ashenvale and the Wetlands. Questing.wiki has the chain order without a route. [Bind on Forever Duskwood](https://bindonforever.com/zones/duskwood), [Questing.wiki Duskwood](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/leveling/duskwood/), [Judgement's Alliance guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/alliance-leveling-guide/).
- **Why one zone.** The route so far stays in the eastern human zones, so it takes the Duskwood quests that open at 17-20 in one block instead of Judgement's continent hops. Ashenvale and the Wetlands fit later in milestone 3.
- **Loop 1** (about 21-23):
  - Bind in Darkshire.
  - Tranquil Gardens skeletons for The Night Watch. Their Skeleton Fingers are also for the Totem of Infliction.
  - West through the crossroads to Raven Hill, then the western spiders for Watcher Dodds, then Sven's Camp.
  - The dire wolves on the slopes toward the hermit (the spawn data puts them at 15-35, 24-27, between Lars and Abercrombie).
  - Back past the crossroads and Sven's farm mound to Darkshire.
  - The comb errand, with Black Widow Hatchlings north of Blind Mary's house (84, 53) for the crab cakes.
- **Loop 2** (about 24-25): Visit Sven and Abercrombie, then hearth to town and turn in Zombie Juice (158) to unlock Gather Rot Blossoms (156).
- **Loop 3** (about 25-27): Bring the crab cakes to Jitters and finish the Shadowy Figure errand. Clear Skeletal Fiends and Horrors once for The Night Watch (57), Rot Blossoms (156), and any Skeleton Fingers still needed for The Totem of Infliction (101). Finish ghoul fangs in the cemetery, then take the Sven and Darkshire turn-ins before Juice Delivery and Ghoulish Effigy. The local Zygor guide likewise groups the fiends and horrors with the blossoms. [Rot Blossom prerequisites and drops](https://bindonforever.com/quest/156)
- **Wetlands handoff.** Below level 26, the guide offers remaining Duskwood quests or the Stockade as catch-up XP. The next travel waypoint takes players back to Darkshire for the flight. [Blizzard's dungeon XP hotfix](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dungeon-enemy-xp-increased-november-8/2406595/1)

## Left out for now

- **Seasoned Wolf Kabobs (90):** needs Stormwind Seasoning Herbs from Stormwind.
- **Look To The Stars (174-177):** needs a Bronze Tube from Engineering or the auction house.
- **Messenger to Darkshire (145/146):** needs Messenger to Westfall (144), which the Westfall section doesn't take.
- **Ira's Dagger (96137, Forever):** its giver, Bath'rah the Windwatcher, has no Duskwood position in the data.
- **Level 28 and later** (Worgen in the Woods, Night Watch 3, The Legend of Stalvan, Ogre Thieves, the Ebonlocke translation chain): these go in a later Duskwood visit.
- **The Valor Family (96139)** is a Forever quest. Its required level (shown as 18) and its search spot in Raven Hill need the recorder.

- Whether the Raven Hill Tome from Memory of Valor is on the broken table around 21.2, 55.7, as recorded. [Valor objective](https://bindonforever.com/quest/96139)
- Real levels at the end of each loop.
