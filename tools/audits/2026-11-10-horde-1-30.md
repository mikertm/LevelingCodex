# 2026-11-10: Horde 1-30 quest route

## Method

- **Backbone: RestedXP's Forever Horde guides** (the owner's local copy of RestedXP Guides v4.11.35):
  `Guides/forever/Horde-01-12_Durotar.lua`, `Horde-01-14_Undead.lua`, `Horde-1-12_Mulgore.lua`,
  `Horde-12-22_Barrens.lua`, the dungeon variants under `Guides/forever/dungeon/`, and the Forever Horde 22-30
  and dungeon chapters inside `Guides/forever/RestedXP-Skyborne.lua`. We take quest IDs, the grouping of quests
  into loops and their order. Step text is ours; RestedXP's wording is never copied.
- **Facts: QuestieDB (Forever data and traces)** via `tools/questfacts.py`, which now knows the Horde zones and
  takes `--faction horde` for zone listings. NPC positions come from QuestieDB spawns; objective areas from
  RestedXP's waypoints, converted from world yards to map percentages with the client's `UiMapAssignment`.
- **Client check:** every quest ID is checked against `tools/reference/forever.json` by the route audit.
  Three quests RestedXP's chapters mention are not in the Forever client and are left out: 813 Finding the
  Antidote, 926 Flawed Power Stone, 972 Water Sapta.
- **Web research:** bindonforever.com zone pages (quest lists with Forever-new flags), Warcraft Tavern's Forever
  leveling pages, questing.wiki and Wowhead Forever. Each zone's notes list the pages used.
- **Simplification:** RestedXP has separate paths per class (vendor runs, softcore/hardcore variants, grinding
  targets, class training). We keep one route for everyone, with class quests on the quest (`q(..., class)`)
  and trainer visits expanded per class with `withTrainers`.
- Scratch tooling (session scratchpad, not shipped): a RestedXP chapter parser, a draft generator that joins a
  chapter's order with QuestieDB facts, and a world-to-map coordinate converter.

## Durotar (Orc and Troll, 1-12)

RestedXP chapters: "1-6 Durotar", "6-10 Durotar", "10-12 Durotar". RestedXP sends most classes to Tirisfal and
Silverpine at 10-12 and keeps Hunters and Shamans in Kalimdor. We follow the Kalimdor path for everyone, which
leads straight into the Barrens.

- **Valley of Trials:** RestedXP's order. Class letters are picked up from Gornek at the start and handed to the
  trainer right away, as on the Alliance side. Forever adds two letters, Glyphic Parchment (98576, Orc Mage) and
  Tainted Tablet (98575, Troll Warlock). The Warlock imp chain (1485, 1499) and the Shaman Call of Earth chain
  (1516-1518) stay in the route for their class. Burning Blade Medallion is picked up by everyone after Vile
  Familiars, so Warlocks take it a few minutes later than RestedXP has them do.
- **Camping:** The Adventurer (96652) starts from the Lost Journal by Zureetha and leads to Brakk's campfire south
  of Razor Hill: The Great Outdoors (96604) and Camping 101: Cooking (96655). The other Camping 101 profession
  intros are left out.
- **Sen'jin and Razor Hill:** RestedXP picks up the Razor Hill quests early and hearths back to the valley. We walk
  Sen'jin first (Kolkar Crag, Pal'juh's escort) and then go north through the Ridgeshade hills to Razor Hill,
  which needs no hearth and covers the same ground. Forever quests on this loop: Legging It (96821), Lost in the
  Shadows (99123), Bloodtalon Matriarch (97223), Forgotten Loa Idols (97225), For Honor (96822), This Fruit Could
  Bite Back (96825), A Missing Hand and Threat from Below (99048, 99049).
- **Elite and rare drops:** Ukor's Lost Pack (Ukorsbane), Halikor's Hoof (Halikor) and A Simmering Storm (Dull
  Storm Orb) are `startItem` quests: the steps show only once the item is in your bags.
- **Left out:** A Pain in the Neck (96873) needs Enchanting to disenchant the pendants, so it is optional (Settings
  > Show optional quests). Threat from Below (99051, Naga Spinefins) is not in RestedXP's route and its area is
  unconfirmed. Need for a Cure (812) needs Finding the Antidote (813), which the client lacks. Troll Priest's
  Garments of Spirituality (5648) stays in for that class.
- **Orgrimmar:** two visits. The first (level 10) delivers the Admiral's Orders to Nazgrel, gets Hidden Enemies
  from Thrall and the flight path. The second (level 12) does Neeru's Skull Rock chain and Forever's Orgrimmar
  errands (Meal Appeal, Favorite Food, Yelmak's Medley, Whuut's the Rush, Rocks to Rests; quest level 15, short
  city errands RestedXP includes). Hidden Enemies' follow-up (5727) is part of the Ragefire Chasm detour.
- **End:** the bridge into the Barrens, where Kargal and Ak'Zeloth give Crossroads Conscription (842) and The
  Demon Seed (924) for the Barrens route.
