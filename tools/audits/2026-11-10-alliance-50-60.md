# Alliance route 50-60 (QuestData5060.lua)

Date: 2026-11-10. Status: unverified in game (every section has `verified = false`).

## Sources

- Order and quest grouping: the Alliance 50-60 chapters of a reference guide (speedrun set "50-50 Stranglethorn Vale"
  through "59-60 Winterspring/Silithus part 2", compared with its dungeon-guide copies). Facts and order only; every
  step text is our own wording.
- Quest facts (IDs, names, levels, givers, enders, prerequisites, objectives, item IDs, NPC spawn points):
  QuestieDB Forever data at revision 0f7d8ad via `tools/questfacts.py` (cached dump), checked against the client
  quest list in `tools/reference/forever.json`. Flight path names come from the same reference (`TaxiNodes`).
- Coordinates: quest givers and enders from QuestieDB spawn data; objective areas from the reference chapters'
  world positions converted with `UiMapAssignment` (scratch tooling, not shipped). Where the two disagree on an
  NPC (Tirion Fordring) the reference's Forever position is used.
- Zone pages: wowhead.com/forever zone pages listed in each section's `sources`.
- Mount Hyjal: no published quest list could be confirmed (wowhead now serves a verification page; the guide and
  news sites found only describe the zone and its two flight paths). No Hyjal section was written.

## Loop check

Script: a turn-in or pickup whose NPC the route passed within 40 yd once it was possible, then walked more than
150 yd away and came back. Result after fixes: 6 flags, all accepted:
- blasted-51 2601/2603/2585/2583: the bloodmages' item quests are accepted and handed in on separate visits by design
  (the hunt is in between).
- ungoro-53 3884: an item-started quest (the journal drops anywhere).
- plaguelands-57 5537: Skeletal Fragments is picked up on the second Chillwind visit on purpose (quest-log cap).
The alternates' first versions had two loops (Morgan's Vigil pickups, Umi in Everlook); fixed by grouping the pickups.
