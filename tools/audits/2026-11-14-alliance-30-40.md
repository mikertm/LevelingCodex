# Alliance route 30-40: loops, gaps, elites, dungeon chains and shopping (2026-11-14)

Second pass over the Alliance 30-40 chapters in `QuestData3040.lua` (first pass: `2026-11-10-alliance-30-40.md`),
part of the Alliance 1-60 audit that follows the Horde one (`2026-11-14-horde-1-60-audit.md`). This file covers
the main chapters `darkshire-30`, `hillsbrad-32`, `needles-33`, `desolace-35`, `stv-36`, `swamp-38`, `arathi-39`,
`dustwallow-40` and the alternates `stv-30`, `theramore-33`. The two Riverglades alternates in the same file belong
to the 40-50 pass. Every chapter stays unverified until it is played.

## Sources

- Quest facts: the QuestieDB Forever cache (commit 0f7d8ad) through `tools/questfacts.py`: givers, enders, levels,
  `requiredLevel`, prerequisites, item drops and vendors, NPC and object spawns. Every new coordinate is a QuestieDB
  spawn or cluster centre unless the step carries an `uncertain` note.
- Forever client reference `tools/reference/forever.json`: every new quest ID is in the client; flight links
  (Refuge Pointe to Southshore) from `QuestFlightData.lua`.
- Reference guide A (RXP Forever, the "(A)" chapters 29-32 Duskwood to 40 Dustwallow Marsh in
  `RestedXP-Skyborne.lua`): quest IDs, grouping, order and positions only, never text. Positions taken from it,
  converted with `ns.questWorld`: Fozruk's patrol line (44.5, 58.8), Grel'borg's patrol start (agrees with QuestieDB),
  Skonk (57.7, 74.7) and the Boulderfist ogre camp (53.8, 74.6), Father Tuttle's escort end at Magtoor (agrees with
  QuestieDB). It also confirmed that the elites in this band are routed for everyone with "skip if you can't"
  warnings, and that Archmage Ansirem stands at 14.2, 59.7 by the redrawn Dalaran (QuestieDB still has the classic
  18.8, 78.5; Archmage Modera's image is at 14.3, 59.6 in QuestieDB too, so the route keeps guide A's spot).
- Zygor Forever guides (quest-ID gap check only).
- Wowhead Forever: [Packaged Pristine Pelts 98156](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98156) (ends at Kimberly
  Grant, part 2 of Pristine Pesterhide Pelts 98155), [Carved Centaur Totems 93224](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93224)
  (ends at Captain Pentigast, no starter listed).
- Method: `tools/questloops.py ALLIANCE HUMAN,DWARF,GNOME,NIGHTELF,SKYBORNE` with all nine classes, the coordinator's
  gap, carried, elite, item and buy lists, and the quest log simulated per section with the route audit's own rules
  (all races and classes, dungeons and optional steps off and on) after every change.
