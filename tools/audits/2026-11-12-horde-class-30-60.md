# Horde class quests 30-60 (2026-11-12)

New side sections in `QuestHordeClass3060.lua` (loaded after the other `QuestHordeClass*.lua` files and
before `QuestClassData.lua`). No route, dungeon or `Data.lua` file changed. Nothing here is verified in
game yet (`verified = false` on every section, set by `B.hordeSide`).

## Sources

- **Which chains and in what order:** reference guide B's Horde "<Class> Class Quests" guides (Druid,
  Priest, Warrior, Hunter, Mage, Rogue, Shaman, Warlock) and the class-filtered steps of reference guide A's
  Horde 30-60 chapters (30-33 Hillsbrad/Arathi through 56-58 Western/Eastern Plaguelands). Quest IDs and
  order only; every step text is our own and no waypoint was copied.
- **Quest facts** (level, minimum level, class and race masks, givers, enders, prerequisites,
  objectives, item drops, spawn clusters): QuestieDB Forever 0f7d8ad through `tools/questfacts.py` and a
  scan script over the same cache. Coordinates are QuestieDB spawn points or the centre of a mob's spawn
  cluster.
- **Client reference** (`tools/reference/forever.json`): every quest ID used is in `quests.ids`; flight
  names are exact taxi node names. Checked absent: 1714 Essence of the Exile, 4785 Fine Gold Thread,
  6701 Syndicate Emblems.
- **Scan:** every QuestieDB Forever quest with a class mask, open to Horde races, with a quest or minimum
  level of 26 or more. It found the chains below plus level-60 raid and dungeon-set content (skipped).
  Forever-added quests (IDs 70000+) carry no class masks, so they were listed by name at level 28+: the
  only class content among them is the shaman weapon chain (Horde 79360, 79364, 79361, 79366, 79442;
  Alliance 96264, 79358, 96135, 96016).
- **Web** (Forever-only facts QuestieDB lacks):
  - [Wowhead Forever: Heed My Call (79366)](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=79366): ends at Rau
    Cliffrunner, series of five, provides Corrupted Totems.
  - [Wowhead Forever: The Tempest's Weapons (79442)](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=79442): ends at
    Bath'rah, weapon choice.
  - [conquestcapped: WoW Forever shaman weapon quest](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-shaman-weapon-quest/):
    chain order, totem zones and mobs, the Ravaging Tempest fight.
  - [questing.wiki: paladin class quests](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/classes/paladin/) and
    [shaman class quests](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/classes/shaman/): no Horde paladin quest at
    30-60; shaman list.
  - [Warcraft Tavern: Undead paladin](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/undead-paladin/) and
    [conquestcapped: WoW Forever paladin mount](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-paladin-mount/):
    the Forsaken warhorse at 40 exists but its quest is unconfirmed.

dungeon agents set on the same guides.

## Skipped, with reasons

- **Paladin (Undead):** no Horde paladin class quest between 30 and 60 in QuestieDB or the web sources.
  The Alliance warhorse (1661) and Mightstone (8415-8418) chains are Human/Dwarf only. Forever gives
  Forsaken paladins their own warhorse at 40 and a charger at 60, but no source confirms the quest name,
  giver or ID (one guide reports a datamined name, "A Trusty Steed", with no database page). Open question.
- **Level 60 epic mounts and raid chains:** Warlock Dreadsteed (7562-7631), the paladin charger, Hunter
  Rhok'delar (7632-7636), Mage Arcane Refreshment (7463, Dire Maul), Priest Eris Havenfire (7621, 7622),
  Shaman Darkreaver (7667, 8258, Scholomance), Dire Maul class books (7498-7506), Zul'Gurub class quests,
  Ahn'Qiraj (Armaments of War 8377-8382, rings, cloaks, weapons), Naxxramas sets (not in the client),
  and the dungeon set 0.5 upgrade (An Earnest Proposition 8913-8920, Just Compensation, Saving the Best
  for Last, Anthion's Parting Words): raid, 60 dungeon or 60 epic content, as in the Alliance audit.
- **Warlock Enchanted Gold Bloodrobe** (4736/4739 referrals, 1796, 4781-4784, 4786): 4784 needs Fine Gold
  Thread, whose quest (4785) is not in the Forever client, and 1796 needs crafted Robes of Arcana. Same
  decision as the Alliance.
- **Shaman 8411 Mastering the Elements:** Bath'rah's own copy of Elemental Mastery. QuestieDB requires
  8410 for Spirit Totem (8412), so the guide follows 8410 only.
- **Warlock infernal path** (4963 Shard of an Infernal, 4975 Orb of Noh'Orahil): mentioned in the acolyte
  step, not routed.
- **Mage 2861/2846 Tabetha's Task / Tiara of the Deep:** not class-restricted in the data (any class can
  take it); Zul'Farrak content.
- **Rogue 8249 Junkboxes Needed** (repeatable) and **6701 Syndicate Emblems** (not in the client).
- **Reference guide A's other class-tagged steps** (684, 1049, 2585, 2581, 3161, 7722, 8552, 615, 969,
  675/701, 673, 652...): not class quests in the data; the reference only routes some classes
  differently.
- **Shaman Call of Water / Call of Air hand-ins** that reference guide A carries in its 28-35 chapters:
  levels 20-30, already side guides (`shaman-water-totem-horde`, `shaman-air-totem-horde`).

## Gaps and decisions

- **The Horde shaman weapon chain's last two links** are not in QuestieDB: Heed My Call (79366) has no
  ender there (Wowhead names Rau Cliffrunner) and The Tempest's Weapons (79442) has no record at all.
  The guide hands 79366 in to Rau and takes 79442 from him, with notes. The client also has quest taxi
  paths "Shaman - Level 40 Quest - Thousand Needles - H" (and a flight back): the steam pool event may
  include a flight.
- **Skyborne Horde shamans:** their Call of Air (96136) has no giver in QuestieDB; whether Prate offers
  them Elemental Aid is unknown. The weapon guide has no race filter.
- **Twisted Totem objective order:** the parts use QuestieDB's objective list (1 water, 2 earth, 3 fire,
  4 air), which differs from the description's order; each part carries a note.
- **Essence of the Exile (1714)** is not in the client; kept as in the Alliance guide, with a note.
- **Western Plaguelands way in** for Spirit Totem: north through Alterac past Chillwind Camp (noted), or
  through the Bulwark from Tirisfal.
- **The Alliance has the same weapon chain** (94789 Call of Air to Veyric Thunderhame, then 96264,
  79358, 96135, 96016); `QuestClassData.lua` only routes Call of Air. Worth an Alliance side guide later.
