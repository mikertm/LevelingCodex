# Alliance special quests

These detours can be started independently of the leveling route. Their quest IDs and waypoint maps are in the Forever client reference (`1.60.1.70862`). The cached Forever quest records supply NPC and object positions, quest levels, and prerequisites where present. Each section is marked for live verification. [QuestieDB Forever records](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/tree/0f7d8adc92b28ac6594f4ad8745257f4d9784edf/data/Forever)

## Seeking Caitlin

At level 24, Llana in Astranaar offers 95737 to Caitlin in Menethil Harbor. The detour links to the Excavation Site checklist and becomes obsolete when Lost in the Thicket Things is in the log or finished. Check the offer and the ferry handoff in game. [Forever quest record](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95737/seeking-caitlin) · [Dungeon quest sources](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/every-quest-in-excavation-site-wetlands-wow-forever-383239)

## Cozy Sleeping Bag

- The six-quest Alliance chain is 79008, 79192, 79980, 79974, 79975, 79976. The object traces place the Alliance starter at Alexston Farmstead in Westfall, the first turn-in and Nailed Plank in the Field of Giants in the Barrens, and the remaining objects at the Stonetalon camp and mound, Stonewrought Dam, and Thoradin's Wall. Published Forever sources confirm the level 14 minimum. The quests themselves are level 22 to 32. Rekindle is a separate campfire activity. [Forever quest route and rewards](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/cozy-sleeping-bag-locations-rewards) [Forever minimum and levels](https://wowf.io/en/quests/cozy-sleeping-bag)
- The route uses the Booty Bay to Ratchet boat and gives low-level travelers landmarks for going around Grom'gol, Camp Taurajo, the Crossroads and Sun Rock Retreat. It uses the Menethil to Southshore boat to approach the Thoradin's Wall cart from Hillsbrad. The cart is at 87.36, 49.62 on the Hillsbrad map. The bag and satchel are on the Arathi map. The dam figurine is on the ledge below the top, facing the Wetlands. [Forever travel routes](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/all-travel-routes-in-wow-forever-boats-zeppelins) [Dam and wall landmarks](https://oraclenewsnetwork.com/guides/cozy-sleeping-bag)
- Method's Stonetalon camp pin differs from the Forever object trace and ONN. The route uses the trail entrance near 50.9, 52.3 and the object trace at 40.77, 52.57. Reports agree on the Alliance Old Toolbox reward, a 12-slot bag. Some item listings also show the Sturdy Lunchbox, while faction-specific guides give it to Horde, so the route asks the player to check the reward window. The sleeping bag's Well-Rested buff grants 1% bonus experience per stack, up to 3%. [Method walkthrough](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/how-to-unlock-the-cozy-sleeping-bag-in-wow-forever) [Faction rewards and trail entrance](https://oraclenewsnetwork.com/guides/cozy-sleeping-bag) [Reward comparison and buff](https://forevermastery.com/en/guides/wow-forever-sleeping-bag)

## The Legend of Stalvan

- All 13 parts start at level 22. The first 12 have a quest level of 28 and run from Madame Eva through Moonbrook, Goldshire, Stormwind, Eastvale and Darkshire. The level 35 finale sends the player to level 32 Stalvan at Manor Mistmantle. The route calls out the Forlorn Spirit encounters and suggests coming back in the low 30s or with a group for the last fight. [Opening quest](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=66/the-legend-of-stalvan) [Moonbrook footlocker](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=67/the-legend-of-stalvan) [Goldshire chest](https://bindonforever.com/quest/70) [Finale](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98/the-legend-of-stalvan)

## The Missing Diplomat

- The 17-part chain starts with Thomas at level 28 and runs through the final audience with Jaina (1267). The Stormwind and Duskwood legs fit around level 28. Trias's Menethil lead is a pause point for a group or level 30-34 before Tapoke. Mikhail's Theramore lead calls for level 35-38 or help with the finale. The Forever cache gives levels 26, 34 and 35 for Dashel, Tapoke and Hendel. [Chain order and fights](https://theforeverguides.com/quests/the-missing-diplomat/) [Docket objective](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1244/the-missing-diplomat) [Tapoke objective](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1249/the-missing-diplomat)
- Walk into the Sentry Point tower for exploration credit on 1265. Players report Tervosh at the tower or back in Theramore. At the end of 1324, Tervosh and Jaina can arrive at the camp, while their static positions in the Forever records are in Jaina's tower. The route gives both locations. [Sentry Point](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1265/the-missing-diplomat) [Hendel event](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1324/the-missing-diplomat) [Jaina turn-in](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1267/the-missing-diplomat)
