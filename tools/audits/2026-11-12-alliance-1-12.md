# 2026-11-12: Alliance starts 1-12 and the Horde Skyborne copy: loops and gaps

Sections: `northshire`, `elwynn`, `coldridge`, `dunmorogh`, `shadowglen`, `teldrassil` and the Skyborne `zephras`, `shendar`, `valanaar` (the shared `zi` table) in `QuestData.lua`, plus the Windshaper copy of Zephras Isle in `QuestHordeSkyborne.lua`. This pass is modelled on `2026-11-12-wetlands-early-dig.md`. Every chapter is still unverified.

## Why

In the Wetlands the owner found a quest offered by an NPC the route already visits but never routed, and a pickup placed after a walk through its own objective area. This pass looks for the same faults in the starting zones: late pickups, revisited hubs, objective areas crossed twice, and quests other Forever guides take that we don't.

## What was wrong

1. **Northshire.** The Nibbled-On Book drops from the Kobold Vermin, but its use step came after McBride's turn-in. The arrow sent the player back to the vermin (181 yd).
2. **Elwynn.** After Brackwell the route walked back east to Sara Timberlain only to hand in Red Linen Goods, then went west to Jasperlode. The second eastern loop stops at Sara anyway, for Cloth and Leather Armor. That cost about 470 yd. The loop check can't see this kind of fault: a turn-in made early when a later visit to the same NPC would serve.
3. **Dun Morogh.** Kharanos was crossed back and forth on all three visits:
   - first visit: Beldin, Bellowfiz, Stonegear, then Loslor back by Beldin;
   - second visit: Ragnar, then east to the depot, then back to Senir;
   - third visit: Senir, the depot, west to Tognus and Razzle, then back to the depot for Beldin.

   Together that cost about 490 yd.
4. **Teldrassil.**
   - Kyra was visited on the second Dolanaar loop only for The Lost Runner, though she stands beside the inn on the first.
   - Fel Rock (north-west of Dolanaar) came before the Starbreeze Mystics (east), so the player crossed Dolanaar twice before heading west to the den, which lies past Fel Rock.
   - On the last Darnassus visit, Eyes of the Sentinels was taken in the temple. The pendant tour then crossed the city to the bank, the Enclave, the inn and the gate, came back to the temple, and went out again to the Craftsmen's Terrace and the portal. That is about 3,800 yd of city walking, against about 1,700 yd for one loop.
5. **Horde Skyborne.**
   - The Horde route had no Zephras Isle size, so the arrow could only show yards if the client's map API answered, and the loop check couldn't measure the island.
   - In Shen'dar, the High Order camp (45.8, 39.3) lies on the path to the bandit cave, but the apprentices were fought after returning to the village. That cost about 450 yd (cave to the nests: 1,421 yd before, 968 yd now).
   - Shamans end the orchard trip at the Valanaar brazier, yet still got the "hearth to Valanaar" step. A Shaman could spend the hearthstone that is needed after Shadowgale.
