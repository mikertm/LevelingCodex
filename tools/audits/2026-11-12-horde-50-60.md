# Horde route 50-60 (QuestHorde5060.lua)

Date: 2026-11-12. Status: unverified in game (every section has `verified = false`).

## Sources

- Order and quest grouping: reference guide A's Horde 50-60 speedrun chapters ("50-51 Stranglethorn/Blasted Lands"
  through "59-60 Winterspring/Silithus II") and its dungeon-flavoured copies (which outdoor pickups feed the Sunken
  Temple, Blackrock Depths, Stratholme and Dire Maul); reference guide B's Horde 50-60 chapters for a second order
  (Searing Gorge, Burning Steppes and Azshara at 51-52, Felwood and Winterspring at 52-53, Un'Goro at 53-54). Facts
  and order only: every step text is our own wording, no reference coordinates were used.
- Quest facts (IDs, names, levels, minimum levels, givers, enders, prerequisites, objectives, item IDs, NPC and object
  spawn points, exploration triggers): QuestieDB Forever at revision 0f7d8ad through `tools/questfacts.py` (batch
  lookups in scratch scripts), checked against the client quest list in `tools/reference/forever.json`. Flight names
  and Horde flight master spots come from the same reference (`TaxiNodes`) and QuestieDB.
- Coordinates: givers, enders and objects from QuestieDB spawns; kill and drop areas from the centre of the busiest
  QuestieDB spawn cluster of the creature that drops the item. Road points between maps are converted with the
  client's map world positions (`quests.maps[id].world`).
- Zone pages: the wowhead.com/forever zone pages listed in each section's `sources`.
- Mount Hyjal: no Horde quest list could be confirmed. Pages read: https://expcarry.com/wow-forever-mount-hyjal-guide
  (names only "To Have Loved and Lost" and "Friendly Advice", no givers or IDs), https://metaforge.app/wow-forever/zones/mount-hyjal
  (403), a search that surfaced a beta-era page naming Cataclysm quests. The client reference has one Hyjal quest
  POI (91564 "To Have Loved and Lost", 15.6, 50.0) and QuestieDB has no Hyjal quests, so no Hyjal section was written.

## Gaps and decisions

- Threat of the Winterfall 5082: QuestieDB lists Trouble in Winterspring 6603 (Meggi Peppinrocker, Everlook) as its
  prerequisite; both reference guides take 5082 at Donova without it. We follow QuestieDB: `felwood-53` rides on to
  Everlook (flight path, 6603, Felnok's letter) and back to Donova before the Winterfall camps. Check in game; if 6603 is
  only a breadcrumb, the Everlook detour can move after Donova.
- Volcanic Activity 4502 is accepted in `blasted-50` (Ratchet). Reference guide A takes it at the end of its 49-50
  Feralas chapter; if the 40-50 file also accepts it, remove one of the two accepts at merge.
- Prerequisites with two choices in QuestieDB use the Horde one on the step: Wasteland 1124 after Rabine Saturna 1123
  (the shared record says 6762, the Alliance chain), Pamela's Doll 5149 after Sister Pamela 5601 (record: 5142),
  Scarlet Diversions 5096 after the Thunder Bluff call to arms 5095 (the alternate uses the Undercity one, 5094).
- Not in the Forever client: Enticing Negolash 619 (the bait step is a buy and the objective text), The Videre Elixir
  4041 (Gregan's Evoroot errand is described in 3909's step), Meeting with the Master 3503 and Return Trip 3421 (the
  Xylem teleports are described), Locked Away 5059.
- Left out for the quest log (the main route peaks near the cap with dungeon pickups on): Better Late Than Never 5023
  and the Jeremiah chain 5049/5050/5051, Andron's Payment to Jediga 3564 (needs another Azshara trip), Winterfall
  Activity 8464 in the main route (kept in `winterspring-alt`), Return to Tinkee 4810.
- Left out for missing data: Aquementas 4005 -> Linken's Adventure 3961 -> It's Dangerous to Go Alone 3962 (QuestieDB
  has no Aquementas spawn; the Linken chain stops at Silver Heart 4084); Past Endeavors 5057 (QuestieDB ends it at
  Storm Shadowhoof while the text names Melor Stonehoof).
- Left out on purpose: cloth donations (7813-7836), the Light's Hope 30-item quests (9124-9136), Salve via Hunting
  5882/5887 (repeatable), Runecloth 6031, Taking Back Silithus 8276 (QuestieDB marks it exclusive with 8280), A Tale of
  Sorrow / The Swamp Talker 2801/2623 (prerequisite from the 40s), Seed of Life 7066 (Maraudon), Jammal'an 1446,
  The God Hakkar 3528, Luck Be With You 969.
- Class quests from the reference chapters are left to the class files: Warrior 8423-8425, Warlock 8419/8421/8422,
  Mage 8250-8253, Priest 8254-8257, Hunter 8151/8153/8231/8232, Druid 9051-9053.
- Dungeon pickups tagged (`H.dungeon`): Sunken Temple 1445 (Fel'zerul, Stonard; needs 1444), 3446/3447 (Marvon; need
  3444); Blackrock Depths 3906 Disharmony of Flame (Thunderheart, Kargath); Dire Maul East 5526 Shards of the Felvine
  (Rabine, last step of the route). Left to the dungeon guides because a pickup with no outdoor turn-in holds a log
