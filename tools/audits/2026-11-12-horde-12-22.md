## What was wrong

1. **Silverpine had two quests with no objective step.**
   - Wild Hearts (429): accepted from Rane Yorick, turned in at Renferrel.
   - Wild Eyes (91920, Forever): accepted from Quinn Yorick, turned in at Renferrel.

   The route never sent the player to the worgs or the murlocs, so it arrived at Renferrel with nothing to hand in. Both are in the alternate too. A script over every accept in these sections (QuestieDB objectives, no `complete` step anywhere in the route) found only these two. Blood Shards of Agamaggan is handed in from shards already looted.
2. **The loop check's late pickup for Ivar the Foul (425) came from wrong data.**
   - Our `q(425)` named Return to Quinn (430) as its prerequisite. QuestieDB's Forever fixes say Return to Quinn (Again) (91921), which matches the Forever chain 430 -> Wild Eyes -> 91921 -> Ivar.
   - Bind on Forever and guide A both take Ivar after 91921.
   - The route's order was right; the prerequisite is corrected and the finding is gone.
3. **A zig-zag the tool can't see: Grimson the Pale.**
   - The route finished Arugal's Folly (423) shackles on the Lordamere shore at 56.5, 46.1. Grimson's mine (424) is 95 yards from there.
   - The route then walked back to the Sepulcher to turn in 423 and take 424, and walked all the way back east to Grimson: about 620 yards each way.
4. **Priest pickups in Thunder Bluff (`sun-rock` 75/76, now 78/79).** Miles Welsh trains Priests, then they walk down to Zamah, then back up to Miles for Shadowguard or Dark Sacrifice. The pickups are inserted by `QuestHordeClassCasters.lua` after the Zamah turn-in. That file is outside this audit (see Outside scope).
5. **Thorn Hill in Forever.** QuestieDB has Chol'aruk (20) and Razormane Berserkers and Flesheaters (elite, 18-19) at Thorn Hill's south-west end, where the crossroads chapter sends level 13-15 players for Kreenig. The danger warning covers the spot. The step text didn't mention it.
