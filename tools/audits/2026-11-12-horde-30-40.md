# Horde route 30-40 (2026-11-12)

New chapters in `QuestHorde3040.lua`, below the shared Horde 30-60 setup. They continue the Horde
main route from `thousand-needles` (28-30, Freewind Post, hearth bound there) and end at Kargath at
level 40 with the hearth bound in Kargath, ready for the 40-50 route's flight to Grom'gol.

## Gaps and decisions

  quests 1104, 1105, 1110 and 1176. Kept both: `flats-33` adds Stormheart, the silithid hive, the
  Swarm chain, Zherin's basilisks and the Ratchet deliveries; a player who did the alternate finds the
  racing steps done. The alternate has no `suggest`; `hillsbrad-30` would fit.

  1014, 1098 (Shadowfang Keep), 6563 and 6921 (Blackfathom Deeps), still in the log from the 22-30

visit, Mus'hale's quest taken between the flight master and the inn. No zig-zags were reported for the
30-40 sections.
