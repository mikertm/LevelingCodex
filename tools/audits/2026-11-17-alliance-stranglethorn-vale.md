# Alliance Stranglethorn Vale audit

Reviewed the Duskwood approach, the early Stranglethorn alternate, both main jungle visits, the level-50 Booty Bay return, and the handoffs to Darkshire, Southshore, the Riverglades, and Theramore.

The Alliance guides gained 36 quest IDs, including 20 Forever entries. The shared quest records for Wendel and Zimmix also correct their displayed quest levels in the Horde guide, to 38 and 35 respectively.

## Route changes

| Visit | Findings and changes |
| --- | --- |
| 30–35, Rebel Camp and Nesingwary | Pick up Jungle Secrets at Thorsen's normal camp position, with his road patrol as a fallback. Turn it in before the first hunts. The early alternate takes the Small Brass Key on the surface and leaves the Stockpile strongbox for the return with Special Forces. |
| 36–38, Kurzen and the west coast | Get the Venom Fern Extract from the surface supplies. Turn in both starter quests to unlock Special Forces, then do the Commandos, Headshrinkers, Wendel's cage key and uniform, and Herod's strongbox on one Stockpile visit. Added Cozzle's key and footlocker directions. The first Haunted Isle visit is an exploration objective. Follow the coastal tablet, Bloodscalp, shipment, key and escort objectives before returning to Booty Bay. Pick up Krazek's ore and crock-pot follow-ups while passing his balcony and return the pot on the second Rebel Camp visit. Ratchet's optional Velinde chain continues through Ruzzgot, the Darkshire mine and Darnassus. The Desolace campfire chain now reaches Tokal and the Cherry Grog vendor. |
| 41–43, Booty Bay and the interior | Pick up the naga reeds and gorilla jobs before the northern cove outing, then finish Shaky's deliveries. Group Swashbucklers, both Bloodsail objects, snuff and the glass eye at the Wild Shore. Cortello's scroll is below deck on a ship. Do Zanzil's mixture and all three named targets before accepting Maury's ogre follow-up. Use Catelyn's Blade at the Vile Reef altar and return to Revilgaz. Crank takes the mixture and Whiskey Slim gives a separate quest. Group Skullsplitter tusks and fetishes, then stack the Mosh'Ogg objectives for Mai'Zoth, Noise Complaints and Maury's key. Barnil's book and four chapters are optional. |
| 41–43, Forever outdoor quests | Added Gnaz's four crude oil drums and Knaz's ogres. Added Ruzzgot's Bloodsail trunk, Venture Co. ledger, agent and shipment return for the Riverglades route, plus Lozgil's replacement shipment to Whizzbuzz. The two mystical scroll drops lead to Dudd on the Booty Bay return. |
| 35–40, The Drowned City | Added the optional Alliance dungeon checklist. It follows Troll Witchery to Nimetz's report, Dibbs's two quests, Timear's idol pickup and Krazek's two pirate quests, then covers Sunken Hollow, all six client encounter IDs and the post-run reports. The Booty Bay route links to the checklist at Krazek. |
| 50, southern shore | Mok'rash supplies the Monogrammed Sash. Smotts and Sprogger lead into Negolash with the food and wine hand-in at the lifeboat. The three Bloodsail captains have individual waypoints. Open the Half-Buried Bottle on the mainland before swimming to Poobah. Gorlash is on the beach north of Jaguero Isle. |

The coverage pass focused on Alliance and neutral leveling quests. Fishing contest repeatables, PvP arena and pirate reputation quests, professions and level-60 raid or reputation chains belong to separate activities. The handoffs already resident in the route include Goblin Sponsorship, Stoley's Debt, Whiskey Slim's Lost Grog, and Akiris by the Bundle.

The Rumormonger, Dream Dust and Fool's Stout circuit stays outside the main itinerary because it requires repeated deliveries between the Shimmering Flats, Booty Bay and the swamp. Seahorn's last Sunken Treasure letter would send the player back to Faldir's Cove after the sample turn-in. The existing itinerary leaves that return off the route. Unbagwa's fang hand-in and the ruined lifeboat's bait hand-in are instructions within their parent quests. The committed Forever client reference lacks their separate Classic quest IDs (`349` and `619`). The Booty Bay route uses the Smotts quest IDs with current giver records (`8551`–`8554`).
