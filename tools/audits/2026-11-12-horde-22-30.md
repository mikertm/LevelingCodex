# 2026-11-12: Horde 22-30, loop and gap pass

Sections, all in `QuestHorde22to30.lua`: `sepulcher`, `hillsbrad`, `camp-taurajo`, `stonetalon-sunrock`, `ashenvale-splintertree`, `thousand-needles` and the alternate `shimmering-flats`. Follows `2026-11-10-horde-22-30.md`, `2026-11-10-horde-1-30.md` and `2026-11-10-horde-loop-audit.md`. Every section stays unverified.

## Why

This is the Wetlands dig audit (`2026-11-12-wetlands-early-dig.md`) applied to the Horde 22-30 chapters. It looks for two things. The first is walking: a pickup or turn-in at an NPC the route already stands next to, and objective areas or towns visited twice when one visit would do. The second is gaps: quests the reference guides or QuestieDB place on our walk that the route doesn't take.

## What was wrong

1. **Fly steps sat at the departure flight master.**
   - Every `fly` step in this file used the flight master you leave from. The rest of the route, and the engine (`Q.flightStep`: "a Fly step sits at the destination's flight master, so it stays current until you land"), use the destination.
   - With the departure spot, the step counted as done the moment you reached the flight master. The flight map's mark, travel automation and the "Fly to X" step text were therefore gone before the taxi map opened.
   - All 13 fly steps now use the destination: a new `fm` table of flight masters, plus `tbPlaces.tal`. Each step's text names the flight master you leave from.
2. **Weapons of Choice (893)** was optional and told players its weapons came "from the northern Barrens". All three drop in the southern Razormane camps that `camp-taurajo` clears for Kuz, Lok and Nak. Its follow-up A New Ore Sample (1153) was dropped with it.
3. **The Sacred Flame (1195)**: the moonwell step expects an Etched Phial in your bags, but no step said where it comes from (Laughing Sisters at Raynewood).
4. **Stonetalon zig-zag.** The chapter landed at Sun Rock, rode east to Ziz and Windshear Crag, then came back west through Sun Rock to go north. At the end it rode east past Ziz again to reach Malaka'jin.
5. **Thunder Bluff, both visits.** They went Zamah (below Spirit Rise), then Hunter Rise or the central rise, then the class trainer. Shamans, Paladins, Priests and Mages train on Spirit Rise, so they crossed the city to Spirit Rise twice.
6. **Freewind turn-ins after a visit to the same NPCs.** At the end of the western loop the route stood at Nyse, the flight master, about 10 yards from Elu and 70 from Jandia, with Wind Rider (4767) and both Jandia quests (98069, 98070) complete. It flew to Thunder Bluff and handed them in on the next Freewind visit, after the level 30 training.
7. The first Ashenvale step said "Fly to the Crossroads", but `stonetalon-sunrock` ends at Seereth on the Stonetalon pass, which has no flight master.
