# Leveling Codex

In-game companion for **The Leveling Codex** WoW: Forever leveling guides. It traces the talent to take each level (on the Codex guide's path, a recommended path for any spec, or a custom path you build), reminds you what to train, and sets up smart macros in one click. The Professions tab has 1–300 guides for Mining, Blacksmithing, Tailoring, Enchanting, Herbalism, Skinning, Leatherworking, Alchemy, Engineering, First Aid, Cooking, and Fishing, with a crafting window trace for guided recipes. Open it with `/codex` or the minimap book.

Optional integrations coordinate quest automation, place the step window beside your tracker, and point the arrow at nearby unfinished objectives. Compatible UI skins can also supply the window frame, panels, accent colour and font.

The Quests tab opens a journey board with connected area map previews and one selected quest panel. The previews use the client's map artwork and explored terrain; route cards show their zone, and dungeon cards label their entrance-area map. Browse six quest cards at a time within an area. The side rail opens route and dungeon tiles, quest-log categories, party panels and Options. Route previews leave your followed step alone; dungeon pickup plans stay separate from entering a run. Options groups route choices, display controls, helpers and party settings into focused pages.

The **Loot** tab (`/codex loot`) finds equipment by slot, level and source, compares item links
and bag items with equipped gear, and tracks where to get upgrades. Profiles follow talent
paths or use your own weights and share codes. Tooltip advice, a separate gear-upgrade reward
marker, enchant suggestions and an optional target tracker use the same comparison rules.
Scores are advisory; unknown facts and unsupported effects remain labeled for review.

The **Journey** tab is a storybook of your adventure that writes itself as you play: chapters by level in a narrator's voice, your trail on the map, trophies and the companions you met. At the auction house, five Codex tabs (Codex, Market, Deals, Quick Sell, Scan) add a market view with charts and a ticker, a deal sniper, one-click selling with protections and a record of every trade; it works with Auctionator. The **Gold** page tracks your mount fund and gold by zone.
